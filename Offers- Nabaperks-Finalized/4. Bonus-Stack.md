**Nabaperks Bonus Stack**:

### Bonus 1: Print-Ready Marketing (Physical First Run)

- **The Obstacle Solved:** "I have no time or designer for posters."
- **What It Is:** Lapen Inns generates the A4 counter posters with the venue QR and counter copy, and then physically prints and posts the first counter-poster run directly to the pub.
- **The Replacement/Comparison Value:** ~£150+ (which is what a freelance designer and a local print shop would charge to produce and fulfill this).

### Bonus 2: Birthday Automation — Configured

- **The Obstacle Solved:** "I will never remember to message birthday guests."
- **What It Is:** You configure the birthday reward name and terms on their card so that the platform's daily automated job issues birthday rewards during a customer's birthday month.
- **The Replacement/Comparison Value:** The hours of time saved every single week by removing the manual chase of messaging customers by hand.

### Bonus 3: Referral Engine — "Bring a Regular" Configured

- **The Obstacle Solved:** "I need new diners without using paid ads."
- **What It Is:** You configure the referral sharing mechanics so existing members can easily invite new diners. The system automatically handles attribution and bonus-stamp settlement for the referrer when the new diner makes their first genuine visit.
- **The Replacement/Comparison Value:** A ready-to-go referral loop, saving them the immense hassle of inventing and tracking a "guest-bring-guest" scheme from scratch.

### Pro-Tip for Delivery

Hormozi advises that when selling 1-on-1, you can use these bonuses strategically. You present your core offer and price. If they hesitate, you present a bonus that directly addresses their specific perceived obstacle (e.g., "I don't have a designer... well, as a bonus we actually print them for you"), and then ask if that makes it "fair enough". People have a hard time rejecting this kind of reciprocity.
