This process transforms a commoditised software subscription into an irresistible, high-value system by identifying every obstacle your prospect faces and solving it through your deliverables.

### Step 1: Identify the Dream Outcome

- **The Framework:** The dream outcome is the expression of the feelings and experiences the prospect has envisioned. You must focus on them arriving at their destination.
- **The Nabaperks Application:**
  - **Ultimate Destination:** A live, no-app loyalty card that captures the pub’s existing weekend customers and helps bring them back during quiet mid-weeks—without needing a customer app, POS integration, or extra work at the till.
  - **The Fast Win:** Within the Done-For-You (DFY) launch window, the owner gets an immediate emotional win by seeing their venue profile live, card configured, rewards ready, and physical counter posters on the way.
  - **The Transformation:** **Before:** Busy weekends, quiet mid-weeks, and no systematic way to bring customers back. **After:** A working browser card, visible QR posters, automated birthday touches, and a dashboard showing verified returning regulars.

### Step 2: List the Problems

- **The Framework:** Write down every point of friction, limiting thought, or obstacle the prospect will experience immediately before, during, and after using your product. The more problems you find, the more you get to solve.
- **The Nabaperks Application:** From the perspective of the gastropub owner, the obstacles are:
  1. "I have no time to set up another piece of software."
  2. "I do not know what rewards to offer without wrecking my margins."
  3. "My customers will not download another app."
  4. "I do not want a complex POS/EPOS integration project."
  5. "My staff will forget anything complicated at the till."
  6. "I have no designer to make marketing posters."
  7. "I will not know if anyone actually came back."
  8. "If it does nothing in the first month, I do not want to keep paying."

### Step 3: The Solutions List

- **The Framework:** Transform every problem from Step 2 into a solution using "How to" language. You must solve _every_ perceived problem to give the prospect permission to purchase.
- **The Nabaperks Application:**
  1. How to get a complete launch without DIY configuration.
  2. How to start with a food-led mystery reward pool instead of a blank page.
  3. How to enrol guests via a QR code into a browser card.
  4. How to launch without POS/EPOS work.
  5. How to keep the floor process to a simple scan and a short script.
  6. How to receive print-ready posters without hiring a designer.
  7. How to see visits, regulars, and redemptions directly in a weekly digest.
  8. How to keep the pilot free until a first regular returns.

### Step 4: Create Your Solutions Delivery Vehicles ("The How")

- **The Framework:** Determine exactly _how_ you will deliver these solutions using the "Delivery Cube" (e.g., 1-on-1, 1-to-many, DIY, DWY, DFY).
- **The Nabaperks Application:** To maximise value and minimise the pub owner's "Effort & Sacrifice," you will use a **Done-For-You (DFY)** delivery model. You do the heavy lifting of the initial configuration (venue, card, rewards, printing) so they only have to place the posters on their counter and brief their staff.

### Step 5: Trim & Stack (The Final Core Offer)

- **The Framework:** Look at the cost of providing the solutions. Remove high-cost/low-value items. Keep the high-value/low-cost items, and select a few high-value/high-cost items to ensure they get results.
- **The Nabaperks Application:**
  - **Trimmed (Excluded):** Unlimited consulting, monthly 1-on-1 strategy calls, ongoing reprint budgets, multi-site rollouts, paid ads management, and POS integrations. These are high-cost operational drags that kill margins.
  - **Stacked (Included):** DFY venue/card configuration, 5-stamp cycle, mystery pool, permanent QR, first physical A4 poster run, dashboard, and the First-Regular Guarantee.

---

### The Final Core Offer Stack: "The 30-Day Gastropub Mid-Week Revenue Accelerator"

**Core Component 1: Done-For-You Venue & Card Launch**

- Venue profile and branded browser loyalty card configured for the pub.
- Recommended 5-stamp food-led stamp cycle configured.
- _Why it is valuable:_ It removes the "blank account" problem; the pub starts from a working setup, not a DIY project.

**Core Component 2: Food-Led Mystery Reward Engine**

- Custom weighted mystery reward pool configured (e.g., free starter, coffee after lunch, Sunday roast upgrade) that is margin-safe.
- _Why it is valuable:_ Solves the headache of "what should we give away" without resorting to blanket discounting.

**Core Component 3: No-App QR Capture System**

- Permanent venue QR provisioned with customer join/stamp journeys tested.
- No customer app, no wallet-pass requirement, no POS integration.
- _Why it is valuable:_ Fits perfectly into busy counter-service and removes the biggest guest objection (app fatigue).

**Core Component 4: Merchant Visibility Loop**

- Dashboard access for visits, members, stamps, and returning customers.
- Weekly digest of visits, regulars, and redemptions sent to the owner.
- _Why it is valuable:_ The owner can judge the ROI from their own concrete numbers without guessing.

**Important Claims Boundary:** You are selling a guided, live system designed to encourage and measure return visits. You must **never** guarantee midweek revenue or filled tables.
