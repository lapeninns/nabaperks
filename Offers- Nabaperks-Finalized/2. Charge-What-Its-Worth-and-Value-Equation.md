**Pricing: Charge What It’s Worth**

The fundamental principle here is to **never compete on price**—doing so commoditises your offer and forces a "race to the bottom". Instead, Alex Hormozi advocates charging a premium price to create a **Virtuous Cycle of Price**.

When you raise your prices, you increase your clients' emotional investment and perceived value (as proven by blind wine taste-tests where people rate the exact same wine higher simply because it costs more). Higher prices also multiply your margins, allowing you to deliver an exceptional service that actually gets them results.

**How this applies to Nabaperks:**
Instead of selling a cheap "DIY loyalty software" login, you are selling a premium **Done-For-You (DFY) service layer**.

- **The Price Architecture:** **No setup fee.** The done-for-you implementation, card configuration, and first physical poster run are included. The Growth Plan is **£49/month** (or £490/year) after the 30-day free pilot.
- **The Price-to-Value Discrepancy:** To justify charging this, you must create a massive gap between what they pay and the value they get. For Nabaperks, if an average return visit yields a £12 gross contribution, the pub only needs **~5 additional return visits per month** to completely cover the £49 software cost. That is roughly one extra return visit a week—making it an absolute steal for the operator.

**The Value Equation**

To justify your premium pricing, you must maximise the value of your offer. The Value Equation is the definitive formula for this: **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort and Sacrifice)**.

Your goal is to increase the variables on the top and drive the variables on the bottom as close to zero as possible. Here is exactly how the Nabaperks offer perfects this equation:

**1. Dream Outcome (Goal = Increase)**

- _The Concept:_ The gap between a prospect's current reality and their ultimate destination.
- _The Nabaperks Application:_ You are not selling "loyalty software." The dream outcome is **making quiet midweek capacity more productive** by giving existing weekend customers an easy reason to return.

**2. Perceived Likelihood of Achievement (Goal = Increase)**

- _The Concept:_ People pay for certainty. How confident are they that this will actually work for them?.
- _The Nabaperks Application:_ You maximise this by doing the setup for them. Lapen Inns configures the system, selects margin-safe mystery rewards, and delivers the physical printed posters so the QR code is guaranteed to be visible. Furthermore, the **First-Regular Guarantee** and the merchant dashboard (showing verified return visits) give them mathematical certainty that the system is working.

**3. Time Delay (Goal = Decrease)**

- _The Concept:_ The time between a client buying and receiving the promised benefit. Fast beats free.
- _The Nabaperks Application:_ By using a Done-For-You launch, you completely remove the pub owner's "DIY setup queue". They get an immediate emotional win: a fully branded, live, displayable programme delivered as a launch package, without having to spend weeks figuring out the software themselves.

**4. Effort and Sacrifice (Goal = Decrease)**

- _The Concept:_ What ancillary costs, time, and physical/mental friction must the prospect endure to get the result?.
- _The Nabaperks Application:_ This is Nabaperks' biggest technical advantage. You drastically reduce the operator's and customer's effort because there is **no customer app to download**, **no POS/EPOS integration project**, and **no complex till processes** for the staff. You even remove the effort of finding a graphic designer by printing the first poster run for them. The only effort left is displaying the materials and briefly training the staff.
