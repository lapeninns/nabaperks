### 1. The Final Niche Statement

**Target Niche:** Single-site, owner-operated UK food-led pubs with strong weekend trade, quiet Tuesday–Thursday periods, and no effective digital loyalty programme.

**The Niche Promise:** Nabaperks sets up and launches a no-app browser loyalty card for a single-site UK food-led pub, so the venue can capture existing weekend customers and encourage measurable return visits—without a customer app, POS integration, or extra work at the till.

---

### 2. The "Starving Crowd" Market Indicators

According to the framework, a market is only worth pursuing if it meets four core criteria. Here is exactly how Nabaperks aligns with them:

- **☑ Massive Pain:** Your prospects suffer from quiet Tuesday–Thursday service, which leaves rent, staffing, and kitchen capacity painfully underused while overheads remain high.
- **☑ Purchasing Power:** Established food-led pubs have strong weekend cash flow and can assess a £299.99 done-for-you launch plus £69.99 every 28 days against measurable return visits. The platform is free during the first 28 days; the annual plan is closed to new customers.
- **☑ Easy to Target:** You do not have to guess where your prospects are. They can be found instantly through Google Maps, Instagram, booking platforms, CAMRA, local hospitality networks, and trade directories.
- **△ Growing (The Pivot):** While the overall UK pub market is not rapidly expanding, the _demand_ for simple, measurable retention tools is. By specifically targeting commercially healthy operators rather than failing pubs, you position yourself in a pocket of growth.

### 4. Qualification vs. Disqualification Rules

To ensure you maintain high profit margins and do not waste time on clients who are a bad fit, you must apply strict rules for who you accept.

**Prioritise venues that:**

- Are single-site and owner-operated (or owner-led day-to-day).
- Serve food Tuesday to Thursday.
- Have visible weekend demand and a meaningful existing customer base.
- Have one manager willing to own the launch and ensure QR counter materials are displayed.
- Want the implementation "Done-For-You", rather than just buying empty software access.

**Ruthlessly disqualify venues that:**

- Are closed most Tuesdays to Thursdays or lack an existing customer base.
- Expect you to guarantee midweek revenue or promise filled tables.
- Will not display the physical QR codes or refuse to briefly train their staff.
- Need multi-site rollout, bespoke agency creative, or POS/EPOS integration within the standard done-for-you launch.
