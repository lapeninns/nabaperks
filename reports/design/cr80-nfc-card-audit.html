Audit notes

  1. 1
    True-size output

    Each printed page is 85.5 × 54 mm. Disable “fit to page” and print at 100% scale.

  2. 2
    Safe zone

    Critical copy and the QR sit at least 3 mm from the finished edge. Guides are screen-only.

  3. 3
    NFC keep-out

    The left-centre 28 × 30 mm zone stays visually quiet. Confirm the exact antenna footprint with the chosen card stock.

  4. 4
    QR fallback

    20.8 mm frame, pure white ground, high contrast, and ECC-H. The encoded URL is a pilot placeholder.

  5. 5
    Copy lock

    Venue leads. Tap instruction, browser support, low-friction line, and card number remain inside the physical safe zone.
