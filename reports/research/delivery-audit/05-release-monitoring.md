# Release and monitoring evidence

Audit date: 2026-09-21. Method: read-only audit using the verified findings supplied for this task; no repository behaviour was changed. The citations below are valid for both the local tree and `origin/main`.

## A. Promotion gate sequence and revision binding

- Preflight admits only a successful CI `workflow_run` from `main`, or a dispatch where `EXPECTED_REVISION = $GITHUB_SHA` on `refs/heads/main` (`.github/workflows/production-database.yml:4-7,38-45`). It then requires successful push-event `ci.yml` (one attempt) and `codeql.yml` (up to 60 retries at 10 seconds) for the exact revision (`.github/workflows/production-database.yml:47-76`). This is gate-existence checking, not a tip check.
- The tip-of-main guard is `test "$(git rev-parse origin/main)" = "$EXPECTED_REVISION"` after a full-depth checkout: staging `.github/workflows/production-database.yml:109`, baseline `:253`, promote (job at `:339`, step “Verify immutable revision and protected credentials” `:374-377`), and deploy `.github/workflows/production-deploy.yml:113`. A candidate superseded on `main` is rejected before `supabase db push` (`.github/workflows/production-database.yml:420`).
- Promote binds `EXPECTED_REVISION` to `workflow_run.head_sha || inputs.expected_revision` (`.github/workflows/production-database.yml:346`), checks out that ref, and binds ledger identity to revision, run ID, and attempt (`scripts/release/stage-ledger.mjs:164-171`).
- The runbook acknowledges that advancing `main` during approval can invalidate the guard (`docs/operations/production-runbook.md:318-321`).

## B. Concurrency and stuck-state hazards (including the evidence-expiry interaction)

- Evidence expires after one hour. `maxAgeMs = 3_600_000` is the default in `validateStageManifest` (`scripts/release/manifest.mjs:77-80`) and `qualifyRelease` (`scripts/release/stage-ledger.mjs:185-188`). The assertion requires `completedAt <= now && now - completedAt <= maxAgeMs`, otherwise reporting “stage evidence is stale or from the future” (`scripts/release/manifest.mjs:110-112`). The same rule applies to compatibility evidence (`scripts/release/compatibility.mjs:7`) and stage transitions (`scripts/release/manifest.mjs:123-124`).
- Every `verifyLedger` invocation enforces the fresh clock and recurses through the full chain (`scripts/release/stage-ledger.mjs:294`). Promote reads the ledger in “Verify qualification on the fresh protected runner” (`.github/workflows/production-database.yml:391-400`) and again before writes (`:408-418`). Deploy re-verifies repeatedly (`.github/workflows/production-deploy.yml:160-177`, inside stage `:256-258`, promotion `:326-330`, verified `:529-545`).
- A failed assertion prints “Release stage ledger verification failed; advancing the release is forbidden.” and exits 1 (`scripts/release/stage-ledger.mjs:510-513`), before database or application mutation.
- An approval or queue wait longer than one hour therefore fails cleanly at the first fresh ledger verification; it cannot use stale evidence. The runbook says to start a fresh complete outer run and never redate a stale ledger (`docs/operations/production-runbook.md:318-321`).
- A separate concurrency hazard is that approval can outlast the candidate’s position at the tip of `main`; the tip guard rejects the superseded candidate rather than allowing it to promote.

The `application` reusable call (`:441-461`) has no caller-level timeout; its callee owns the timeout: deploy preflight 2 (`.github/workflows/production-deploy.yml:37`) and deploy 30 (`:80`). `production-smoke.yml` probes have timeout 7 (`:31`); incident and resolve-incident have none.

Environment-approval waits occur before job runtime, so the repository does not explain a hold exceeding the configured runtime timeout.

## D. Rollback (documented vs automated) and the recovery drill

- Rollback is manual. For the application, the runbook requires identifying the last healthy production deployment before promotion (`docs/operations/production-runbook.md:28`), running the passwordless guard `node scripts/check-passwordless-rollback-revision.mjs "$HEALTHY_GIT_SHA"` (`:382`), then `vercel rollback "$HEALTHY_DEPLOYMENT_ID" --yes` and `vercel rollback status nabaperks` (`:395-396`), followed by `/api/health` and `/api/readiness` probes (`:397-399`).
- Database rollback is forward-only: never edit or delete an applied migration; add a compensating migration, prove it on a disposable database, and deploy through the normal gate (`:399-403`). Never restore a full backup over live data without incident-owner approval (`:403-405`).
- Nothing automates rollback in `.github/workflows` or `scripts/`. The workflow “rollback” references are the staging proof’s rollback-only loyalty journey (`.github/workflows/production-database.yml:176`) and disposable-target rollback compatibility proof (`scripts/release/populated-upgrade.mjs:547`, `scripts/release/compatibility.mjs:51`). `check-passwordless-rollback-revision.mjs` is a manual pre-rollback guard.
- `recovery-drill.yml` is dispatch-only, with `backup_id`, `restore_project_ref`, and the literal confirmation `VERIFY_NON_PRODUCTION_RESTORE` (`.github/workflows/recovery-drill.yml:6-16`). Preflight requires `main`, a 20-character ref different from production `skonlhwstejberyzobep`, and a numeric backup ID (`:27-41`).
- The protected “Recovery Drill” verify job does not restore. The operator first performs Supabase Restore-to-a-New-Project (`docs/operations/production-runbook.md:409-447`). The job captures backup/project listings, materialises independently reviewed lineage/source-manifest JSON, then runs `scripts/check-restored-backup.mjs` (`.github/workflows/recovery-drill.yml:101`). That script read-only verifies the restored ledger, forced RLS, seven core tables, and three core RPCs (`scripts/check-restored-backup.mjs:7-23`). Evidence is uploaded as `recovery-drill-<backup_id>` for 365 days (`.github/workflows/recovery-drill.yml:104-108`). It had zero runs in the 30-day window.

## E. Production smoke wiring

- Triggers are schedule `cron: "7/15 * * * *"` (`:5`), `workflow_run` for completed “Production database promotion” with no branches filter (`:7-8`), and dispatch with optional `expected_revision` (`:10-13`). The job applies the branch condition in `if`.
- The probe job (`:27-85`) checks unauthenticated `GET https://nabaperks.com/api/health`, asserting status `ok`, scope `liveness`, service, ISO time, 12-character revision, and production environment/target environment (`:74-77`). It checks `/api/readiness` with `Bearer $PRODUCTION_MONITOR_SECRET`, asserting `ready`, database and operational checks, eight cron jobs, queue-age/failure-rate signals, and the revision pin (`:78-81`). Latency is checked by `node scripts/check-production-probe-latency.mjs` against `config/production-slos.json` (`:82-84`).
- On the `workflow_run` path, the candidate is resolved from the bound artifact through `scripts/vercel-production-target.mjs` and `scripts/release/read-candidate-artifact.mjs` (`:45-58`). The latter appends `EXPECTED_REVISION` to `GITHUB_ENV` (`scripts/release/read-candidate-artifact.mjs:250-252`) and accepts `production-candidate-*` or docs-only `production-unchanged-*`; the latter probes the baseline revision and adds a step-summary disclaimer (`read-candidate-artifact.mjs:222-260`).
- A cancelled promotion still triggers `workflow_run` because `completed` includes cancelled, but probes require `conclusion == 'success' && head_branch == 'main'` (`:29`) and therefore skip. Incident requires `probes.result == 'failure'` (`:90`) and resolve requires success (`:141`), so the cancelled path runs with all jobs skipped and probes nothing.
- Dispatch sets `EXPECTED_REVISION` only for dispatch (`:24`). “Wait for the verified revision” polls `/api/health` up to 30 times at 10 seconds (`:60-73`) and pins `.revision == $revision[0:12]`; an empty input leaves the revision unpinned. The runbook keeps manual dispatch available for rollback and incident verification (`docs/operations/production-runbook.md:126-127`).
- Probe failure creates or updates “[Production alert] Nabaperks readiness failure” with `bug`, `area: platform`, and `priority: p0-critical`, then calls `node scripts/notify-production-alert.mjs trigger` with `PRODUCTION_ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL` and `PRODUCTION_ALERT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (`:87-137`).
- Recovery is handled by `resolve-incident` (`:138-232`) only after probe success. For scheduled events it consults `scripts/production-smoke-recovery-core.mjs` to require the previous distinct scheduled run to have completed successfully at attempt 1 with no intervening non-green run (`:179-186`; `scripts/production-smoke-recovery-core.mjs:1-41`), then calls `notify-production-alert.mjs resolve` and closes the issue (`:204-232`). Deployment-triggered or manual success cannot close the incident.
- There are no artifacts: `production-smoke.yml` contains no `upload-artifact`. Smoke is post-hoc evidence and gates nothing.

## F. Database/application coupling

- Promote applies migrations and uploads the stage ledger (`.github/workflows/production-database.yml:420,434-440`). The `application` job needs promote and calls `production-deploy.yml` (`:441-461`) with `expected_revision`, `release_run_id`, and `release_run_attempt`, preserving the same outer run and attempt.
- Deploy downloads the exact artifact `production-database-stage-${release_run_id}-${release_run_attempt}` (`.github/workflows/production-deploy.yml:105-109`), verifies `stage-ledger verify --stage database-applied` recursively back to qualification (`:160-177`), verifies the unchanged live baseline, and re-verifies before staging and promotion (`:256-258,326-330`). Preflight requires `RELEASE_RUN_ID = GITHUB_RUN_ID` and the caller path `production-database.yml` (`:51-71`). Ledger identity is re-derived from Git (`scripts/release/stage-ledger.mjs:482-501`).
- For `application_required == 'false'`, set by `scripts/release/no-deployment.mjs:116` only for `workflow_run` candidates (manual dispatch always forces `true`, `no-deployment.mjs:63-72`), the `production-unchanged` artifact is retained (`.github/workflows/production-database.yml:260-267`) and staging, qualification, promote, and application all skip (`:81,278,341`). There is no database push or deploy; smoke verifies the existing baseline revision.
- An application deployment without same-revision database success is not admitted: artifact naming, ledger identity, caller binding, and Git-derived identity all require the promote stage of the same run, attempt, and revision.

## H. Monitoring and hygiene workflows (purpose, cadence, alert routing, gating power)

### SLO report

- `slo-report.yml` runs `scripts/check-production-slo.mjs` (`.github/workflows/slo-report.yml:34`) with `PRODUCTION_SLO_CONFIG: config/production-slos.json`. It probes no live endpoint: it queries the GitHub Actions API for scheduled `production-smoke.yml` runs (`scripts/check-production-slo.mjs:12-17`) and `slo-report.yml` activation evidence, then computes 30-day availability, coverage, and error budget from smoke conclusions (`calculateAvailabilityReport`, `scripts/check-production-slo.mjs:211-300`). Targets are 15-minute probes, a 99.9% objective, 95% coverage floor, seven-day minimum observation, and a 30-day window (`config/production-slos.json:5-13`). It exits 1 when non-compliant (`:342`).
- States are `warming`, `breached`, or `compliant`; anything else is `error` (`.github/workflows/slo-report.yml:35-45`). On breach/error, it finds or creates the open “[Production alert] Nabaperks 30-day SLO breach” issue with `area: platform` and `priority: p1-high`, commenting on every existing-issue run (`:68-88`, `:85`, `:87`), then pages with `notify-production-alert.mjs trigger availability-slo` using `PRODUCTION_ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL`/`SECRET` (`:89-95`). On compliance it resolves the webhook and closes the issue with a recovery comment (`:96-107`, `:111-116`, `:117-135`).
- The final SLO gate fails unless the SLO step succeeds (`:136-139`), so `warming` fails the run but opens no issue and sends no page. All 31 scheduled runs in the 30-day window failed. A persistent failure produces one standing issue with one comment per run and one webhook trigger per run; the receiver deduplicates already-triggered incidents (`supabase/migrations/20260903130000_production_alert_receiver.sql:124-127,152-156`) and sends email only when `pageRequired` (`supabase/functions/_shared/production-alert-core.mjs:226-234`). A human therefore gets one page email initially, followed by issue comments and red runs until recovery.
- If the webhook is misconfigured, the page step fails after three attempts (`scripts/notify-production-alert.mjs:5,153,221`); the earlier issue step and red run remain. The artifact is kept 365 days (`.github/workflows/slo-report.yml:53-60`).

### Agent watchdog

- `heartbeat` is gated by `vars.LOCAL_CI_WATCHDOG_ENABLED == 'true'` (`.github/workflows/agent-watchdog.yml:19`); its live value is unverified. It reads no file. `scripts/check-agent-liveness.mjs:16-45` fetches check-runs named “Nabaperks Local CI heartbeat” on pinned anchor SHA `9fb41d6cbc8f...` (`config/local-ci-contract.json:320-327`), filters to the pinned App, and requires successful completion within 20 minutes (`config/local-ci-contract.json:324`; `ops/local-ci/core/agent-liveness.mjs:20,38`). The local agent publishes the check run (`ops/local-ci/agent/github-heartbeat.mjs:34-41`).
- `public-health` is deliberately ungated (`:38-41`) and curls `https://nabaperks.com/api/health`, asserting status, scope, service, and environment (`:53-54`).
- `alerts` always runs after both monitors. A skipped heartbeat reaches the reconciler as `null`, meaning paused rather than outage (`:74-76,85-87`); the assignee is `contract.agentLiveness.notificationAssignee = "lapeninns"` (`:90`; `config/local-ci-contract.json:327`). `reconcileWatchdogIncidents` matches bot-authored issues by exact `[Watchdog] <message>` title and hidden marker (`scripts/watchdog-incidents.mjs:10-15`). Paused leaves an open incident alone (`:78-86`); healthy closes with “Recovery observed” (`:88-101`); unhealthy existing incidents are unchanged and silent (`:102-107`); unhealthy with no issue creates one assigned to `lapeninns` and says repeated failures do not add comments (`:108-118`).
- In the 09-17..20 site-down/stale-heartbeat scenario, the first unhealthy run opened “[Watchdog] Public production health probe failed” and “[Watchdog] Local CI agent heartbeat cannot be verified” (`scripts/watchdog-incidents.mjs:1-3,113`), while production-smoke paged and opened/commented its p0 issue. The watchdog has no synchronous channel; GitHub issue-assignment delivery depends on the account’s settings.

### Nightly validation

- `nightly.yml` jobs have timeouts: `cross-browser` 45 minutes (`:36,39`), `cross-browser-gate` one minute (`:104,109`), `mutation` 90 (`:116,123`), `load` 20 (`:144,147`), `load-race` 15 (`:167,171`), and `zap-full` 30 (`:184,187`). It grants only `contents: read` (`:21-22`), has no issues permission, webhook, or notify step, and therefore emits only a red Actions run plus failure-uploaded artifacts (`:52-60`).
- `load-race` runs only when `vars.STAMP_RACE_URL` and `vars.REDEEM_RACE_URL` are non-empty (`:169`); unset values skip it. Only two of 36 nightly runs succeeded in the observed window.

### Factory status

- `factory-status.yml` runs `node scripts/ci/factory-status.mjs --summary` (`.github/workflows/factory-status.yml:37-40`) on workflow dispatch, completed CI/CodeQL/production-database-promotion runs, pull-request events, and cron `17,47 * * * *` (`:3-14`).
- `collectFactoryStatus` is metadata-only: it reads `main`’s SHA, paginates open PRs based on `main`, collects review/CI evidence (`docs/operations/software-factory.md:11-15`), evaluates each PR and release, and aborts if the base moves during collection (`:59-64`). Missing evidence is `unknown`, not readiness (`:17-19`). It appends a markdown decision table to `GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY` and stdout (`:88-93`) and exits 1 if any item is unknown (`:94-95`). It creates no issue, webhook, or PR comment. The human owner/operator is the consumer; the local coordinator notifies the owner on meaningful state changes (`docs/operations/software-factory.md:11`, `:22-26`).

### Hygiene and merge gating

- `codeql.yml` runs on push, pull request, and weekly cron `27 3 * * 1`; `dependency-review.yml` runs on pull requests. They are the two hygiene workflows that do gate merges: the main ruleset requires the check names `Analyze (javascript-typescript)` and `Review dependency changes`.
- `nightly`, `nightly-proof`, `slo-report`, `agent-watchdog`, `factory-status`, `local-ci-shadow`, and `local-ci-trusted-observer` gate nothing. SLO still has its own reporting-run gate described above, but it is not a merge gate.
