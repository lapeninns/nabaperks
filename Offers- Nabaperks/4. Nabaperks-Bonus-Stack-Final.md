# Nabaperks: Final Bonus Stack

## Locked Offer Context

- **Market:** Established, owner-operated UK gastropubs with 1-3 locations, strong weekend trade and underused Tuesday-Thursday capacity.
- **Core offer:** The Nabaperks Midweek Regulars Engine.
- **Investment:** £49 per venue per month with a six-month initial commitment.
- **Initial contract value:** £294 per venue.
- **Bonus strategy:** Add tools, assets and completed outputs that accelerate the result. Do not discount the core offer or bury the operator in training.

## Bonus Design Rules

Every bonus must do at least one of the following:

1. Create a faster commercial win.
2. Remove work from the operator.
3. Make staff execution easier.
4. Protect the venue's margin.
5. Increase confidence through clearer measurement.
6. Solve the next operational problem created by successful midweek growth.

Replacement values are conservative estimates for equivalent calculators, campaign packs, templates and planning tools. They are not claims that every component is normally sold separately at that price.

## Core Offer and Bonus Boundary

Do not assign value to the same deliverable twice.

| Core offer delivers                                                   | Bonus adds                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| A guided Midweek Opportunity Map with the venue's baseline and target | An advanced reusable Break-Even Scenario Calculator |
| A configured digital venue QR and working customer journey            | A library of print-ready QR launch assets           |

In the sales conversation, present the guided target and working digital system as part of the core engine. Reveal the advanced calculator and print-ready asset pack only when stacking the bonuses.

## Bonus 1: Keep-Forever 10-Minute Gastropub Break-Even Scenario Calculator

**Replacement value: £39**

### What It Is

A reusable calculator that shows:

- Gross contribution per eligible return visit
- Monthly Nabaperks break-even visit target
- Required additional visits per week
- Required additional visits per Tuesday-Thursday service day
- Estimated gross contribution at different return-visit volumes
- Reward-cost sensitivity scenarios

The core Midweek Profit Opportunity Map gives the operator one completed commercial diagnosis and break-even target. This bonus is the reusable, keep-forever scenario calculator they can use after onboarding to model changes in contribution, reward cost and return-visit volume.

### Obstacle It Solves

> "I do not know how many additional visits would make this worth paying for."

It turns the vague ambition to fill quiet tables into a specific and believable commercial target. At £12 gross contribution per eligible visit, the £49 monthly fee is recovered at approximately five additional return visits.

# O.P.P. Partnership Bonus Proposals

These are partnership concepts, not bonuses Nabaperks can promise today. Add one to the customer-facing offer only after a written partner agreement confirms the benefit, funding, eligibility, redemption process and expiry.

## O.P.P. Proposal 1: Partner-Only 30-Day Gastropub QR Reprint Credit

**Proposed customer value: Up to £150**

### Partnership Structure

Negotiate with a hospitality print supplier for either:

- Up to £150 credit against the venue's first qualifying reprint order, or
- A partner-funded percentage discount capped at £150.

The printer gains a qualified hospitality customer likely to order future table talkers, posters and seasonal materials. Nabaperks removes future reprint friction without carrying the print cost.

### Nabaperks Referral Revenue

Negotiate a £50-£100 referral commission for each referred venue that completes a qualifying paid order. The customer credit and Nabaperks commission must be funded by the partner's acquisition budget, not recovered through a higher customer price.

# Sales Integrity Rules

- Never call a bonus "free" if its cost has simply been hidden or added elsewhere. Say it is included.
- Do not advertise partner benefits until the partnership is signed and redeemable.
- Disclose material commercial relationships and referral commissions clearly in the offer and partner terms.
- Confirm that a referral commission does not increase the customer's price or weaken the promised customer benefit.
- Record commissions, redemptions, refunds, tax treatment and VAT treatment with appropriate accounting advice.
- Never recommend an unsuitable supplier merely because it pays the highest commission.
- Do not use artificial countdowns or reset scarcity.
- Do not describe projected gross contribution as guaranteed revenue or pure profit.
- Do not imply that First 100 Members is guaranteed.
- State clearly that physical printing, delivery and installation are not included in the £49 subscription.
