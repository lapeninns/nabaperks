# Nabaperks: Naming Master List

## Naming Strategy

The permanent product identity should remain stable:

> **Nabaperks**

The offer wrapper can change without changing the underlying fulfilment:

> **The Nabaperks Midweek Regulars Engine**

The clearest primary version is:

> **The Nabaperks 6-Month Gastropub Midweek Regulars Engine**

This tells the prospect the avatar, goal, interval and container without promising revenue or filled tables.

Use seasonal wrappers as campaign headlines, followed by:

> **Powered by the Nabaperks Midweek Regulars Engine**

This preserves recognition and proof while allowing the front-end campaign to feel new.

# 2. Rhyming and Alliterative Options

These are stickier but slightly less literal. Pair them with a descriptive subheading in sales materials.

## 1. The Gastropub Midweek Club

**Technique:** Near rhyme between "pub" and "club".

**Descriptive subheading:** A guided system for measurable Tuesday-Thursday return visits.

## 2. Midweek Seats, Regular Repeats

**Technique:** Rhyme between "seats" and "repeats".

**Descriptive subheading:** Turn more existing weekend guests into measurable midweek return visitors.

Do not use the headline to imply that Nabaperks guarantees occupied seats.

## 3. The Midweek Member Momentum System

**Technique:** Three-part alliteration.

**Descriptive subheading:** A no-app gastropub retention system for Tuesday-Thursday trade.

## 4. The Regulars Return Route

**Technique:** Three-part alliteration.

**Descriptive subheading:** A guided path from strong weekends to measurable midweek return visits.

## 5. The Tuesday-Thursday Turnaround

**Technique:** Repeated "T" sound.

**Descriptive subheading:** The Nabaperks six-month gastropub retention programme.

Use "turnaround" as campaign language, not as a promise of guaranteed business recovery.

# Naming Guardrails

- Do not use **Free** for a bonus or component funded by the paid £49 subscription.
- Do not use **Guaranteed Revenue**, **Guaranteed Profit** or **Guaranteed Filled Tables**.
- Use **Break-Even Target**, not **Guaranteed Break-Even**.
- Do not name the entire offer "30-Day" or "90-Day" when the initial commitment is six months.
- A shorter interval may be used for a genuine component, sprint or milestone.
- Use **No-App** and **No-POS** only while those claims remain true for the complete customer journey being promoted.
- Pair playful names with a plain-English subheading explaining the actual outcome.
- Keep seasonal campaign deadlines aligned with published activation cycles and genuine availability rules.
- Record which wrapper produced each lead so offer fatigue can be measured rather than guessed.
