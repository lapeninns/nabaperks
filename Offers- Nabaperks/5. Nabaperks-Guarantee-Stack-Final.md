# Nabaperks: Final Guarantee Stack

## Locked Offer Context

- **Market:** Established, owner-operated UK gastropubs with 1-3 locations and quiet Tuesday-Thursday trade.
- **Offer:** The Nabaperks Midweek Regulars Engine.
- **Investment:** £49 per venue per month.
- **Initial commitment:** Six months.
- **Initial contract value:** £294 per venue.
- **Delivery model:** Guided self-service, automated workflows, reusable tools and standardised reporting.
- **Claims boundary:** Nabaperks does not guarantee revenue, profit, customer spend or filled tables.

# Guarantee Design Principles

1. Guarantee platform access, tracking and defined recovery remedies.
2. Measure activity through Nabaperks records.
3. Keep operator conditions minimal and observable.
4. Use low-cost product extensions and tools rather than unlimited manual work.
5. Cap total monetary exposure at two monthly fees, or £98 per venue.

# Measurement Definitions

## Activation Date

The first business day after the first £49 payment clears and the merchant can access the Nabaperks launch flow.

## Public Launch Date

The date the digital card and venue QR are active and the venue confirms that approved QR materials are displayed.

## Verified New Member

A unique, non-test customer membership created through the contracted venue's live Nabaperks journey after public launch. Staff, tests, duplicates, fraud and demonstration data do not count.

## Eligible Midweek Return Visit

A server-recorded visit that:

- Occurs on Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday using the UK business date.
- Belongs to a genuine customer membership at the contracted venue.
- Occurs on a later UK business date than the customer's earlier qualifying venue activity.
- Passes the normal one-per-customer-per-day controls.
- Is not test activity, staff activity, a manual adjustment, referral bonus or known fraud.

## Venue-Specific Visit Target

> **Monthly visit target = £49 / agreed gross contribution per eligible return visit, rounded up to the next whole visit**

At £12 contribution:

> **£49 / £12 = 4.08, rounded up to 5 eligible midweek return visits**

The contribution figure is a planning assumption approved by the venue. It is not transaction-level revenue proof.

# Guarantee 1: The Launch-Ready Access Pledge

## Headline

> **Your launch tools and merchant setup flow will be accessible after activation, or your next payment waits until access is restored.**

## If/Then/Or-What

- **If** the merchant cannot access the guided setup flow, calculator, templates or QR setup because of a Nabaperks-controlled fault lasting more than two business days,
- **then** the next £49 payment is placed on hold.
- **When** access is restored,
- **then** billing resumes on the next normal billing date without back-charging the held period.

## Operator Conditions

- The first payment has cleared.
- The merchant uses a supported browser and a working internet connection.
- The problem is reported through the support channel with enough information to investigate.

## Risk Reversal

The venue does not continue paying while the launch tools are inaccessible. Nabaperks does not guarantee how quickly the operator completes its own setup steps.

# Guarantee 2: First 10 or Month Two Is Free

## Headline

> **Capture ten Verified New Members within 14 days of public launch, or receive one additional subscription month at no charge plus the Activation Rescue Kit.**

## If/Then/Or-What

- **If** fewer than ten Verified New Members are recorded in the first 14 calendar days after public launch,
- **then** Nabaperks adds one subscription month at no charge.
- **And** the venue receives the Activation Rescue Kit:
  - QR placement audit checklist
  - Staff-script refresher
  - Revised acquisition message templates
  - Reward-friction review worksheet
  - A second 14-day action plan

This guarantee may be claimed once per contracted venue.

## Operator Conditions

- The responsible manager completes the 15-minute briefing.
- QR materials are displayed in agreed customer positions.
- The venue remains open for at least two normal weekend service periods during the measurement window.
- The QR remains active.
- Approved rewards are honoured.

## Risk Reversal

The operator receives more time and a structured recovery process without creating a cash-refund obligation or unlimited consultancy commitment.

# Guarantee 3: The Break-Even Visit Bridge

## Headline

> **Reach your venue-specific eligible return-visit target in a rolling 30-day period by Day 90, or receive one additional month at no charge and the 30-Day Midweek Recovery Plan.**

## If/Then/Or-What

- **If** no rolling 30-day period reaches the contracted visit target by Day 90 after public launch,
- **then** Nabaperks adds one subscription month at no charge.
- **And** the venue unlocks the 30-Day Midweek Recovery Plan:
  - Reward review worksheet
  - QR placement review
  - Staff refresher
  - Campaign refresh pack
  - Weekly self-service scorecard
- **If** the target is still missed after the 30-day recovery period despite continued eligibility,
- **then** the venue may cancel any unpaid remainder of the initial commitment without an early-termination charge.

No previously paid fee is refunded.

## Operator Conditions

- Keep approved QR materials visible.
- Keep the venue QR active.
- Complete the staff briefing and requested self-service refresher.
- Honour approved rewards.
- Use the supplied campaign and recovery materials.
- Remain open for at least 75% of normally scheduled Tuesday-Thursday services.
- Keep payments current except where held or credited.

## Risk Reversal

The venue receives time, tools and an exit route if measured behaviour remains below target. Nabaperks' marginal fulfilment cost remains controlled because the recovery process is productised.

# Guarantee 4: The Proof-by-Five Scorecard Promise

## Headline

> **Receive the monthly Midweek Return Scorecard by the fifth business day, or receive a £10 service credit.**

## If/Then/Or-What

- **If** Nabaperks does not provide the standard monthly scorecard by the end of the fifth business day after a complete reporting month for a Nabaperks-controlled reason,
- **then** a £10 credit is applied to the next otherwise payable invoice.

The scorecard includes:

- Verified New Members
- Eligible Midweek Return Visits
- Progress against the venue-specific target
- The approved contribution illustration
- The next recommended self-service action

# Universal Operator Conditions

1. Provide accurate onboarding inputs and contribution assumptions.
2. Nominate one responsible manager.
3. Complete the 15-minute briefing.
4. Display QR materials.
5. Keep the live QR active.
6. Honour published rewards.
7. Trade substantially according to the qualifying opening pattern.
8. Avoid manipulating tracked activity.
9. Keep payments current except where held or credited.

Missed conditions pause the relevant measurement clock. The venue receives written notice and five business days to cure the issue where practical.

# Remedy and Exposure Rules

- Total fee credits and free subscription months are capped at **£98 per venue** during the initial commitment.
- Deferred payments are not back-charged for the inaccessible period.
- The same event cannot create two monetary remedies for the same billing period.
- Credits are not transferable or redeemable for cash.
- Non-monetary tools and recovery materials are still delivered when applicable.
- Cancellation prevents future unpaid fees; it does not create a refund.

# Customer-Facing Guarantee Stack

> First, if a Nabaperks-controlled fault prevents access to the launch system, your next payment waits until access returns.
>
> Second, capture ten verified members within 14 days of public launch, or receive an additional month at no charge and the Activation Rescue Kit.
>
> Third, reach your venue-specific eligible return-visit target by Day 90, or receive another month at no charge and the 30-Day Midweek Recovery Plan. If the target remains missed after recovery, you may leave the unpaid remainder of the commitment.
>
> Finally, your monthly scorecard arrives by the fifth business day or a £10 service credit applies.

# Claims and Contract Boundary

Use:

- "Venue-specific eligible return-visit target."
- "Five visits at the £12 contribution illustration."
- "Verified using Nabaperks records."
- "One additional subscription month at no charge."

Do not use:

- "Guaranteed revenue."
- "Guaranteed profit."
- "Guaranteed filled tables."
- "Guaranteed break-even."
- "We work free until you succeed."

Official CAP guidance says guarantee meaning and limitations should be clear, and objective claims should be supported before publication. See [CAP guarantees guidance](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/guarantees-and-warranties.html) and [CAP substantiation guidance](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/substantiation.html).

This is a commercial offer design, not legal advice. A UK solicitor should review the Guarantee Schedule and customer agreement before publication.
