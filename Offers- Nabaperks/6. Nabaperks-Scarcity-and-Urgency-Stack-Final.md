# Nabaperks: Final Scarcity and Urgency Stack

## Locked Offer Context

- **Market:** Established, owner-operated UK gastropubs with one to three locations, strong weekend trade and quiet Tuesday-to-Thursday periods.
- **Offer:** The Nabaperks Midweek Regulars Engine.
- **Price:** £49 per venue per month.
- **Initial commitment:** Six months, totalling £294 per venue.
- **Delivery model:** Guided self-service, reusable tools, digital assets, automated workflows and standardised reporting.

## The Ethical Rule

The £49 core subscription is scalable. Nabaperks should therefore not claim that only two venues can join each week, that the digital Campaign Vault is running out or that a prospect must buy on the call.

Scarcity is used only where a real finite resource exists. Urgency is tied to genuine launch or seasonal campaign deadlines.

# 1. Honest Scarcity

## Core Subscription

There is no fixed quantity limit on the standard subscription unless system capacity or support evidence later requires one.

Use:

> The core £49 subscription is available while Nabaperks can support new venue activations to its published service standard.

Do not use:

- "Only two places left" without a real capacity limit
- "Nearly sold out" for a digital product
- A fake waiting list
- A countdown timer that resets

## Optional Human-Service Capacity

If Nabaperks later sells personalised setup, physical print fulfilment, bespoke campaign creation or one-to-one consulting, each service may have a separately published capacity limit based on measured delivery hours.

Until those upgrades exist and their capacity is documented, do not advertise scarcity for them.

# 2. Rolling Launch Urgency

## Weekly Activation Window

Use a weekly Monday activation cycle to create a clear start point without pretending access is scarce.

To join the following Monday activation cycle, a venue must complete these steps by **12:00 noon on Thursday**:

1. Accept the six-month subscription terms.
2. Pay the first £49 invoice.
3. Submit the required venue information.

If the venue misses the cutoff, it enters the next Monday activation cycle. The price and included deliverables do not change.

## Suggested Timeline

| Timing                 | Venue or Nabaperks action                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Thursday, 12:00        | Cutoff for the following Monday cycle                  |
| Friday                 | Automated input check and missing-information reminder |
| Monday                 | Guided Opportunity Map and setup flow unlock           |
| Monday-Tuesday         | Venue configures card, rewards and QR journey          |
| When setup is complete | Digital launch assets become available                 |
| Public Launch Date     | Venue displays QR assets and tracking begins           |

## Why the Deadline Is Real

The cutoff gives Nabaperks time to validate required inputs, provision the venue account, prepare standard assets and protect the launch-access guarantee. Missing it changes the start date, not the £49 price.

## Example

> Complete the agreement, first payment and onboarding information by 12:00 on Thursday 23 July to enter the Monday 27 July activation cycle. If you miss that cutoff, your activation moves to Monday 3 August.

Always replace example dates with live dates.

# 3. Seasonal Urgency

Seasonal urgency must correspond to an actual campaign window. Suitable examples include:

- **New-Year Quiet-Night Reset:** join before the published January activation cutoff.
- **Spring Regulars Revival:** activate before the first campaign week.
- **Post-Summer Midweek Reset:** activate before the autumn campaign calendar begins.
- **Pre-Winter Midweek Momentum:** activate before the published winter preparation date.

The subscription remains £49. The deadline determines whether the venue can participate in that dated campaign cycle; it does not create a fake discount.

# 4. Bonus Urgency

## Bonuses That Remain Always Included

The following scalable assets should not be artificially restricted:

- Break-Even Scenario Calculator
- First-100 Regulars Sprint Kit
- 12-Week Campaign Vault
- Reward Profit Toolkit
- Self-Serve 90-Day Capacity Surge Planner
- Print-Ready Launch-Day QR Conversion Pack

## Legitimate Limited Bonus

Only add a limited bonus when it requires real human labour or third-party inventory. For example:

> The first five venues purchasing the optional Guided Launch upgrade this month receive a one-to-one Capacity Review, subject to five documented appointment slots.

This wording may be used only after the upgrade exists, the five slots are recorded and the limit is consistently enforced.

# 5. Activation Register

Maintain a simple internal register so every deadline is accurate.

## Required Fields

- Venue and location
- Subscription accepted date
- First payment date
- Onboarding information completed date
- Assigned Monday activation date
- Setup status
- Public Launch Date
- Seasonal campaign eligibility, where relevant
- Any optional limited upgrade and its capacity status
- Last-updated timestamp

> Based on what you have told me, you fit the venue this was designed for: you already have weekend customers, and the opportunity is encouraging more of them to return Tuesday to Thursday.
>
> Nabaperks is £49 per venue per month with an initial six-month commitment, so the first six months total £294. You receive the guided setup, calculator, QR assets, campaign templates, staff launch kit and return-visit reporting. There is no app or POS integration.
>
> Our next activation cycle starts on **[Monday, date]**. To enter it, we need the accepted terms, first £49 payment and completed venue information by **12:00 on [Thursday, date]**. If you decide after that, nothing is taken away and the price does not increase; your start simply moves to **[next Monday, date]**.
>
> Would you prefer to begin on **[first date]**, or is **[next date]** more practical for your team?

## If They Need Time

> Of course. The £49 offer is not disappearing today. The only immediate decision is whether you want the **[date]** activation cycle. If you miss Thursday's cutoff, the next start is **[date]**. What question would be most useful for me to answer before you decide?

## If a Genuine Limited Upgrade Exists

> The standard subscription is available either way. The optional **[upgrade name]** has **[number]** documented delivery places remaining this month because it includes individual work. I can only describe that place as available while the capacity register shows it is open.

# 8. Guardrails

Use:

- "The next activation cycle starts on [date]."
- "Complete activation requirements by [time and date]."
- "If you miss the cutoff, the next start is [date]."
- "The standard subscription remains available."

Do not use:

- "You must sign on this call."
- "Last chance" for a recurring subscription.
- "Only one left" without a finite, auditable resource.
- "The price disappears tonight" without a genuine published price change.
- Artificial scarcity on downloadable calculators, templates or campaign assets.

Any public scarcity, availability or deadline claim should be clear, current and capable of substantiation. This is commercial offer design, not legal advice; obtain UK legal review before publication.
