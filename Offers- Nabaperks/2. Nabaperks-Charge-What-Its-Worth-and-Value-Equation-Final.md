# Nabaperks: Charge What It's Worth and The Value Equation

## II. Charge What It's Worth

Nabaperks is designed for commercially healthy, owner-operated UK gastropubs with one to three locations, strong weekend trade and underused Tuesday-to-Thursday capacity.

- **Price:** £49 per venue per month.
- **Initial commitment:** Six months.
- **Initial contract value:** £294 per venue.
- **Delivery model:** Product-led and guided, using calculators, templates, automated setup steps and reporting rather than high-cost ongoing consultancy.
- **What is included:** Guided programme setup, branded browser card, reward configuration tools, venue QR, digital campaign assets, staff launch materials, reporting and reusable optimisation tools.

The pub is not buying a generic stamp card. It is buying an accessible, purpose-built system for encouraging and measuring Tuesday-to-Thursday return behaviour from its existing customer base.

### Price-to-Value Discrepancy

The £49 monthly fee should be compared with the gross contribution the venue could recover through additional eligible midweek return visits.

> **Eligible midweek return visits x agreed gross contribution per visit = estimated gross contribution generated**

For example, if the venue earns £12 in gross contribution from each eligible return visit:

> **£49 / £12 = 4.08, rounded up to approximately 5 additional return visits per month to recover the fee**

That is approximately:

- Just over one additional eligible return visit per week
- One additional eligible visit every two to three Tuesday-to-Thursday service days
- Potentially one additional returning table every few weeks, depending on party size

The central value question becomes:

> **Can Nabaperks help this venue generate at least five additional eligible midweek return visits per month?**

The exact break-even target must use the venue's approved contribution assumption. Five visits is an illustration based on £12 contribution, not a universal revenue or profit claim.

Nabaperks should promise a guided system for increasing and measuring midweek return behaviour. It should not promise filled tables, revenue, profit or a specific customer spend.

### What £49 Can Sustainably Include

At £49 per month, the offer must favour high-value, low-cost fulfilment:

- Reusable calculators and scorecards
- Guided in-product configuration
- Pre-built reward options
- Print-ready digital QR assets
- Recorded 15-minute staff training
- Campaign templates and swipe files
- Automated or standardised reporting
- Self-service recovery and optimisation playbooks

The standard subscription does not include unlimited consultancy, manual monthly campaign management, bespoke design, physical printing or recurring 1-to-1 reviews. Those services may be offered separately only after their delivery cost and price are approved.

## III. The Value Equation

> **Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice)**

### 1. Dream Outcome - Increase

- **Market pain:** Profitable capacity is underused during quiet Tuesday-to-Thursday periods.
- **Dream outcome:** A reliable and measurable base of existing customers returning during quieter midweek periods.

Nabaperks is positioned as a no-app Midweek Regulars Engine designed for established gastropubs, not as generic loyalty software.

### 2. Perceived Likelihood of Achievement - Increase

The selected venues already have strong weekend trade. The system therefore works with an existing customer audience rather than relying entirely on new-customer acquisition.

Confidence is strengthened through:

- A guided pre-launch baseline
- A reusable break-even calculator
- Verified membership and return-visit tracking
- A working QR customer journey
- 30-, 60- and 90-day scorecards
- Standardised performance reporting
- Venue-specific contribution illustrations
- Recovery and optimisation checklists

Strong weekend trade provides the opportunity; clear setup, verified activity and visible milestones provide the certainty.

### 3. Time Delay - Decrease

Nabaperks avoids a lengthy IT project:

- The operator completes the Opportunity Map through a guided calculator.
- The branded card, rewards and QR are configured through a guided launch flow.
- Digital launch assets are available without waiting for bespoke design or printing.
- The staff briefing takes approximately 15 minutes.
- Member and visit activity becomes visible in the merchant dashboard.

The six-month commitment allows time to build behaviour, but the first operational win happens during setup.

### 4. Effort and Sacrifice - Decrease

Nabaperks removes common hospitality technology friction:

- No customer app
- No POS or EPOS integration
- No additional till process
- No blank reward strategy
- No campaign calendar built from scratch
- No lengthy training course
- No raw-data analysis without a scorecard

The venue still needs to:

- Provide accurate information
- Configure or approve its card and rewards
- Display printed or digital QR materials
- Honour the published rewards
- Nominate one responsible manager
- Complete the short staff briefing

At £49, value comes from making expert tools and proven workflows easy to consume, not from assigning a consultant to every venue.
