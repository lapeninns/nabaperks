### I. Pick the right market

Write:

> Established UK food-led pubs and gastropubs seeking more repeat weekday visits

Mark the boxes:

- ☑ **In Pain** — empty Tuesday–Thursday capacity wastes rent, staffing and kitchen costs.
- ☑ **Purchasing Power** — established food-led venues can pay when additional return visits justify the cost.
- ☑ **Easy to Target** — find them through Google Maps, booking platforms, CAMRA, Instagram and hospitality directories.
- △ **Growing** — do not mark this as an unconditional yes. The overall pub market is contracting, although demand for measurable customer retention is increasing.

The “Growing” weakness means we must target commercially healthy operators, not pubs indiscriminately.

### II. Pick a niche

Write:

> Owner-operated UK gastropubs with 1–3 locations, strong weekend trade and quiet Tuesday–Thursday periods, without an effective digital loyalty programme.

Mark:

- ☑ **Niche Down 3–4 Levels**

Use this niche-down ladder:

> Hospitality → Pubs → Food-led pubs and gastropubs → Owner-operated groups with 1–3 locations → More Tuesday–Thursday return visits without an app or POS integration

If the space is too small, write:

> Established owner-operated UK gastropubs with 1–3 sites and quiet midweek trade.

Your complete niche statement remains:

> Nabaperks helps established, owner-operated UK gastropubs with 1–3 locations encourage and measure Tuesday–Thursday return visits—without a customer app, POS integration or additional work at the till.
