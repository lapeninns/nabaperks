# I. Pick the Right Market

## Final Niche

> **Established, owner-operated UK gastropubs with one to three locations, strong weekend trade and underused Tuesday-to-Thursday capacity, seeking measurable customer retention without a customer app or POS integration.**

## Three-to-Four-Level Niche Path

1. Hospitality
2. Pubs
3. Food-led pubs and gastropubs
4. Established owner-operated UK gastropubs with one to three locations, strong weekends and quiet midweeks

## Four Market Indicators

### Massive Pain

The venue already pays for rent, utilities, kitchen capacity and staff while Tuesday-to-Thursday demand remains below potential. The pain is unused productive capacity rather than a vague desire for more loyalty members.

### Purchasing Power

The niche excludes distressed venues with no viable customer base. A commercially healthy gastropub with strong weekend trade can assess a £49 monthly tool against a small number of additional contribution-positive visits.

### Easy to Target

Prospects can be identified through Google Maps, venue websites, Instagram, local hospitality networks, pub directories and trade associations. Qualification requires evidence of food service, independent ownership, one to three sites and a meaningful weekend audience.

### Growing Opportunity

The total pub market need not grow for this offer to work. Nabaperks targets the commercially healthy segment and a persistent operational problem: converting an existing customer base into more frequent visits during quieter periods.

## Qualification Rules

Prioritise venues that:

- Operate one to three locations
- Serve food Tuesday to Thursday
- Have visible weekend demand
- Can honour a margin-safe reward
- Have a manager willing to own the programme
- Can display QR materials
- Want measurable retention without till integration

Disqualify venues that:

- Are closed most Tuesdays to Thursdays
- Lack a meaningful existing customer base
- Cannot honour the advertised rewards
- Expect guaranteed revenue or filled tables
- Will not display the QR or brief staff
- Require bespoke agency services within the £49 plan

# II. Charge What It's Worth

## Price Architecture

- **Single standard price:** £49 per venue per month
- **Initial commitment:** Six months
- **Initial six-month investment:** £294 per venue
- **Multi-site rule:** Each physical venue requires its own £49 subscription because tracking, rewards, configuration and reporting are venue-specific

There is no cheaper DIY tier beside the standard plan. There is also no managed-service promise hidden inside the £49 price.

## Price-to-Value Discrepancy

Use the venue's agreed gross-contribution assumption:

> **Monthly break-even visit target = £49 / contribution per eligible visit, rounded up**

Illustration at £12 contribution per eligible visit:

> **£49 / £12 = 4.08, rounded up to 5 eligible return visits per month**

Five visits is an illustration, not a universal claim. The commercial conversation is whether the system can help encourage and measure approximately one extra eligible midweek return visit per week.

# III. The Value Equation

> **Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice)**

## Dream Outcome

> Build a reliable, measurable base of existing customers who return Tuesday to Thursday, with enough eligible visit behaviour to justify the £49 monthly fee.

The offer is not generic digital loyalty software. It is a Midweek Regulars Engine for a specific venue type and trading problem.

## Perceived Likelihood of Achievement

Confidence increases because the venue already has weekend customers. Nabaperks helps it capture and encourage that audience rather than depending entirely on new-customer acquisition.

Certainty assets include:

- Guided baseline and Opportunity Map
- Break-even target calculator
- Working customer QR journey
- Verified membership and visit tracking
- 30-, 60- and 90-day scorecards
- Venue-specific contribution illustration
- Campaign templates
- Recovery checklists

## Time Delay

The immediate win is clarity: the operator calculates its target and sees the launch path during setup. The digital card, QR and reusable assets do not require a long IT project, app download, POS integration or bespoke print lead time.

## Effort and Sacrifice

Nabaperks removes the blank-page work of choosing a structure, writing every campaign, building training and interpreting raw data.

The venue still must provide correct information, approve or configure its rewards, display QR materials, honour rewards and complete the short staff briefing. The offer is low-friction, not no-responsibility.

# VII. Guarantee Stack

## Measurement Definitions

- **Verified New Member:** A unique, non-test membership created through the venue's live journey.
- **Eligible Midweek Return Visit:** A qualifying server-recorded Tuesday-to-Thursday visit on a later business date than the customer's earlier venue activity, excluding tests, staff, duplicates, bonuses, adjustments and known fraud.
- **Venue-specific target:** £49 divided by the agreed contribution per eligible visit, rounded up.

## 1. Launch-Ready Access Pledge

If a Nabaperks-controlled fault blocks access to the setup flow, calculator, templates or QR setup for more than two business days, the next £49 payment is held until access is restored. The inaccessible period is not back-charged.

## 2. First 10 or Month Two Is Free

If fewer than ten Verified New Members are recorded within 14 days of public launch, Nabaperks provides one subscription month at no charge and unlocks the Activation Rescue Kit.

## 3. Break-Even Visit Bridge

If no rolling 30-day period reaches the venue-specific visit target by Day 90, Nabaperks provides one subscription month at no charge and the 30-Day Midweek Recovery Plan. If the target remains missed after recovery and the venue met the conditions, it may cancel the unpaid remainder without an early-termination charge.

## 4. Proof-by-Five Scorecard Promise

If the standard monthly scorecard is late beyond the fifth business day for a Nabaperks-controlled reason, the venue receives a £10 service credit.

## Operator Conditions

- Provide accurate inputs
- Nominate a responsible manager
- Complete the 15-minute briefing
- Display and maintain the QR
- Honour published rewards
- Use the supplied campaign and recovery materials
- Trade at least 75% of normally scheduled Tuesday-to-Thursday services
- Avoid manipulating activity
- Keep payments current except where held or credited

## Exposure Boundary

- Total free months and fee credits are capped at £98 during the initial commitment.
- Credits are not cash-redeemable.
- Cancellation affects unpaid future fees only.
- No guarantee promises revenue, profit, spend or filled tables.

# VIII. Scarcity and Urgency

## Core Rule

The standard £49 digital subscription is scalable. Do not manufacture a fixed weekly quantity cap.

## Rolling Activation Deadline

To enter the following Monday activation cycle, the venue must accept the terms, pay the first £49 invoice and complete its venue information by 12:00 noon on Thursday. Missing the cutoff moves activation to the next Monday; it does not change the price or remove scalable bonuses.

## Seasonal Deadlines

Use real deadlines for dated campaign cycles such as the New-Year Quiet-Night Reset or Post-Summer Midweek Reset. The deadline controls participation in that campaign window, not permanent access to the subscription.

## Limited Human Upgrades

Only claim quantity scarcity for a launched optional service with measured human capacity or third-party inventory. Keep a capacity register and enforce the published limit.
