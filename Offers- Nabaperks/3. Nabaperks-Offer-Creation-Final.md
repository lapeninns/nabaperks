# Nabaperks: Offer Creation

## Locked Commercial Inputs

- **Niche:** Established, owner-operated UK gastropubs with 1-3 locations, strong weekend trade and quiet Tuesday-Thursday periods, without an effective digital loyalty programme.
- **Niche promise:** Nabaperks helps these venues generate measurable Tuesday-Thursday return visits without a customer app, POS or EPOS integration, or additional work at the till.
- **Price:** £49 per venue per month.
- **Initial commitment:** Six months.
- **Initial contract value:** £294 per venue.
- **Offer type:** A guided, product-led midweek customer-retention system supported by calculators, templates, automated workflows and standardised reporting.

Nabaperks should not make a blanket promise of guaranteed revenue or filled tables. Revenue depends on the venue's footfall, offer, staff participation, service quality, customer behaviour and gross contribution. Nabaperks can credibly promise access to a guided system designed to encourage, measure and improve economically valuable midweek return behaviour.

## Step 1: The Dream Outcome

### Ultimate Destination

> Build a reliable and measurable base of existing weekend customers who return Tuesday-Thursday, with enough eligible return visits to recover more gross contribution than the £49 monthly investment, without an app, POS integration or additional till work.

The exact financial target is agreed from the venue's own economics:

> **Eligible midweek return visits x gross contribution per visit = estimated gross contribution generated**

For illustration, if the agreed gross contribution is £12 per eligible return visit, the monthly break-even target is approximately five additional return visits:

> **£49 / £12 = 4.08, rounded up to approximately 5 eligible return visits per month**

This is just over one additional eligible visit per week. Larger commercial goals may be used only when the venue's baseline, audience size and economics support them.

### The Immediate Fast Win

The operator completes the Midweek Profit Opportunity Map through the guided onboarding flow. Before a customer has joined, the operator knows:

- Their estimated gross contribution per eligible return visit
- Their monthly break-even visit target
- The number of additional visits required each week
- The first customer-capture opportunity to activate

Using the £12 illustration, the operator sees immediately that the programme needs approximately five eligible return visits per month to recover the £49 fee. This is the first emotional win: the commercial goal changes from "fill my quiet pub" into a specific, believable and measurable number.

Any contribution above the break-even target is additional estimated gross contribution under the venue's approved assumptions, not "pure profit" and not a guaranteed result.

### The Transformation

**Before Nabaperks:** The venue has a healthy weekend crowd but no systematic way to identify, incentivise and measure customers returning during quiet midweek periods.

**After Nabaperks:** The venue has a branded, live retention system that captures existing customers, gives them a margin-safe reason to return, records their behaviour and tells the operator what to improve next.

## Step 2: List the Problems

### A. Economics and Strategy - Before Launch

1. **No commercial baseline:** "I have no idea how much my quiet Tuesday-Thursday periods are really costing me or how many extra visits would make this worth paying for."
2. **No clear audience strategy:** "We are busy at weekends, but I do not know how to get those same customers back when the pub is quiet."
3. **Fear of discounting:** "If I start discounting, I will train people to wait for deals and wipe out the margin I am trying to recover."
4. **Reward uncertainty:** "I do not know what reward customers will care about without giving away my bestsellers or making the scheme too expensive."
5. **Low confidence in loyalty schemes:** "I have seen loyalty tools produce sign-ups and pretty dashboards, but I am not convinced they create profitable return visits."

### B. Customer Acquisition - During Launch

6. **No frictionless capture mechanism:** "Weekend service is already hectic. I cannot expect my team to stop and collect customer details before people leave."
7. **Customer app resistance:** "My customers will not download another app, remember another password or keep another paper card in their wallet."
8. **Consent and trust concerns:** "I do not want to mishandle customer data, annoy people with messages or create another compliance headache."
9. **No time for campaign creation:** "I have not got time to write campaigns, brief a designer, find a printer and work out how to launch this."

### C. Staff and Operations - During Delivery

10. **Inconsistent staff execution:** "My staff are too busy to deal with this, they will forget to mention it, and I do not have time to keep training everyone."
11. **Fear of slower service:** "If this adds questions, data entry or even a few awkward steps at the till, the team will stop using it."
12. **Technology anxiety:** "I do not want another IT project, POS integration or system that breaks during a busy service."
13. **Multi-site inconsistency:** "If every venue and shift runs this differently, I will never know what is working or compare the results properly."

### D. Measurement and Momentum - After Launch

14. **No credible return-on-investment view:** "How will I know whether these are genuine Tuesday-Thursday return visits and whether they are worth more than the £49 I am paying?"
15. **Programmes lose momentum:** "This will probably start well, then get forgotten because I do not have time to analyse results, refresh rewards or decide what to do next."

## Step 3: The Solutions List

Each problem becomes a direct solution statement.

1. **How to calculate the exact midweek opportunity and break-even visit target without hiring an analyst or building a spreadsheet.**
2. **How to convert an existing weekend crowd into identifiable midweek regulars without depending on expensive new-customer acquisition.**
3. **How to create a compelling reason to return midweek without blanket discounts or damaging margins.**
4. **How to select rewards customers value without guessing or giving away expensive bestsellers.**
5. **How to prove the programme is producing meaningful return behaviour without relying on vanity metrics or vague marketing claims.**
6. **How to capture busy weekend customers before they leave without forms, queue friction or staff data entry.**
7. **How to let customers join, earn and view progress without downloading an app or carrying a paper card.**
8. **How to capture and use customer permission responsibly without turning the pub owner into a data-protection specialist.**
9. **How to launch an on-brand midweek campaign without hiring a designer or writing every asset from scratch.**
10. **How to make staff execution consistent without lengthy training sessions or complicated operating procedures.**
11. **How to record customer progress without slowing down service or adding work at the till.**
12. **How to launch a digital retention system without POS or EPOS integration, new hardware or an IT project.**
13. **How to roll out the same operating standard across 1-3 venues without rebuilding the programme for every shift or team.**
14. **How to measure eligible midweek return visits and estimated gross contribution without manually combining reports.**
15. **How to improve the programme every month without the owner continuously analysing data or inventing new campaigns.**

## Step 4: The Delivery Cube

### Delivery Principles

- **Group ratio:** Deliver through one-to-many product workflows, templates and recorded guidance.
- **Level of effort:** Use guided self-service and automation. Reserve 1-to-1 work for separately priced future upgrades.
- **Support:** Provide standard business-hours email support and a searchable help system. Do not include scheduled monthly calls or 24/7 support.
- **Consumption:** Combine guided setup, short recorded training, written checklists, calculators, swipe files and standardised reports.
- **Speed:** Deliver the Opportunity Map calculator and digital launch assets immediately. Let the operator complete setup without waiting for a consultant or printer.
- **10x test:** At £490 per month, or £2,940 over six months, add people-intensive managed services. Use these ideas to identify value, not to overload the £49 subscription.

### What the 10x Test Reveals

The most valuable ideas are not necessarily the most expensive. The £2,940 10x version reveals that clients value five things:

1. A commercial target based on their economics.
2. A fully configured system rather than an empty account.
3. Ready-to-use acquisition and staff assets.
4. Clear evidence of progress.
5. Clear guidance about what should happen next.

Nabaperks can reproduce most of this value at low fulfilment cost through templates, configuration workflows, automation, recorded training and standardised reporting. Human time is not included as a recurring standard entitlement at £49.

## Step 5: Trim and Stack

### A. Trim by Value and Fulfilment Cost

#### Keep: High Value, Low Cost

- Venue-specific gross-contribution and break-even calculator
- Preconfigured browser card, reward pool and venue QR
- Reusable print-ready QR and launch assets
- Customer and staff scripts
- Short recorded staff training
- Weekend-to-midweek campaign templates
- Birthday and referral feature configuration where appropriate
- Standardised monthly reporting and in-product progress visibility
- 30-, 60- and 90-day milestones
- Repeatable multi-venue rollout checklist

These items solve important problems and can scale through technology or assets created once and personalised efficiently.

#### Preserve as Optional Future Upgrades: High Value, High Cost

- One 1-to-1 commercial kickoff
- Venue-specific reward strategy
- One live staff launch session
- Monthly 1-to-1 optimisation review
- Manual intervention when performance falls materially behind the agreed milestone

These services are not included in the £49 subscription. They may be offered later as separately priced upgrades after fulfilment costs are validated.

#### Remove from the Core Offer: High Cost, Insufficient Incremental Value

- Weekly on-site consulting visits
- Bespoke POS or EPOS integrations
- Professional photography and video production for every venue
- Physical printing and drop-shipping funded by Nabaperks
- Ongoing reprints and physical installation of campaign assets
- 24/7 phone support
- A dedicated full-time campaign manager
- Weekly live training for every staff shift
- Bespoke legal advice
- Paid advertising management or advertising spend
- Independent financial audits

These can become separately priced add-ons if customers request them. Including them in a £49 subscription would make the model economically unsustainable.

# The Nabaperks Midweek Regulars Engine

> A guided six-month system that helps an established gastropub turn more of its existing weekend crowd into measurable Tuesday-Thursday return visits without a customer app, POS integration or additional work at the till.

### 1. The Guided Midweek Opportunity Map - £49 Value

- Complete a guided commercial calculator.

- Enter the venue's contribution assumption.
- Calculate the monthly visit target.

- Use the guided flow to configure the venue and branded browser card.

**Why it is valuable:** The operator follows a structured launch path rather than starting with an empty account.

### 3. The Weekend-to-Midweek Conversion System - £99 Value

- Provide customer-facing scripts that explain the value quickly.
- Recommend QR placement across the venue.
- Create the initial midweek return proposition.

**Why it is valuable:** It uses the venue's existing happy customers instead of depending on expensive cold acquisition.

### 4. The 15-Minute Team Launch Kit - £49 Value

- A recorded 15-minute staff briefing.
- Reusable access for future starters.
- A one-page shift briefing.
- Simple customer explanation and objection scripts.

**Why it is valuable:** It makes execution repeatable without lengthy training or a complicated operating manual.

- Estimate gross contribution using the venue's approved assumptions.
- Provide a concise standardised performance summary.
- Supply a self-service decision tree when progress is behind target.

**Why it is valuable:** The owner sees structured progress and recommended actions instead of raw activity alone.
