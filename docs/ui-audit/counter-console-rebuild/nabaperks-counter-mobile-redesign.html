Venue console · redesign proposal

Redesign · /app · Old Crown · iPhone SE 375

The venue console is a till tool. Build it at 375 first.

Today it is one 5,590-pixel column of equally loud cards behind a hamburger. This rebuild splits the counter from the numbers, pins the two things a team actually reaches for, and holds its shape from 320 to 1280.

Twelve things that break

Read against the screenshot, not against a heuristic.

01

Navigation is a modal

Seven destinations plus two account items sit behind one hamburger. Every hop at the till is tap → sheet → tap, and nothing on screen says where you are.

Wayfinding
02

Three stacked full-width buttons open the page

Scan code (solid), Announce (outline) and Offers (text) all fire before any content. Roughly 150px of chrome asking three different things at once.

Action economy
03

The QR is the smallest thing on the QR card

A card inside a card inside a card, a ~180px code, “tap to show full screen”, then a separate solid button doing the same job. Four affordances, one task.

One action, one target
04

Two solid vermillion buttons in one viewport

“Scan code” and “Show full screen” both read as the page’s primary. The accent budget is one action per screen, twice at most.

Accent budget
05

The destructive control is the quietest thing on the card

Resetting the team code is gated by a 16px checkbox whose two-line label wraps beside it, then a greyed-out button. A checkbox is not a confirmation, and 16px is not a hit area.

Destructive affordance
06

Four metric tiles, four unreadable sparklines

At 375 each tile is about 160px wide, so the trend line gets roughly 60px. That is decoration wearing a chart’s clothes.

Data density
07

The delta column does not scan

“−8 vs last week” occupies the same slot as “Same as last week”, numbers are not aligned, and direction is carried by colour alone.

Colour alone
08

The 14-day chart has no values

Two overlapping outline series, no y-scale, no labels beyond “2 weeks ago / today”, and no way to read a single point with a thumb.

Filled encoding
09

Recent activity repeats one row four times

Identical badge, identical title, identical full-width “Open QR” button — about 400px saying “someone scanned the QR” and linking back to the QR already on the page.

Signal
10

The perforation is doing separator duty

Wet Ink’s zigzag is a receipt end. Putting it between cards adds a full stop and ~30px after each of the two most-used controls.

One flourish
11

Nothing is pinned

The page is 5,590px of equal-weight column. Whatever you need is wherever you last stopped scrolling — on a phone held one-handed behind a bar.

Thumb reach
12

The ladder only has one rung

Below 768 there is a single column and a hamburger; 320 has no rules of its own, and 768–1024 spends a tablet’s width on the same narrow column.

Breakpoints

The new shape

One console was doing two jobs at two different times of day.

During service the team needs a code to present and a code to read out. After service the owner needs to know whether the week moved. Those are different screens, different postures, different people.

Counter

What the team touches during service, and nothing else. The QR is the tap target — no card-in-card, no duplicate button. The scanner is pinned above the tab bar so it never scrolls away.

  • Present QR · full screen in one tap
  • Team code · reveal, then hide
  • Scan a customer · pinned

Activity & Numbers

What the owner reads afterwards. Repeat events collapse into one countable row you can open. The chart becomes two small multiples with a stepper, because two overlapping series cannot be read with a thumb.

  • Grouped events · open for detail
  • Stamps and joins · separate scales
  • Deltas in words, not colour

More

Everything set up once — poster, announce, offers, members, setup, account. Still a list, but a destination with a title rather than a drawer you have to remember exists.

  • Seven rows at 56px
  • Log out below a receipt rule
  • Becomes the left rail at 900

Breakpoint spec

Six rules, and each one earns its place.

RangeNameWhat changes
≤ 359Compact16px gutters. QR capped at 62vw. Day labels every third column. Delta note drops to its own line. Everything else is identical — the layout does not restructure, it just tightens.
360 – 429Base20px gutters. QR at min(62vw, 236px). Day labels every other column. This is the design target; the other five are adjustments to it.
430 – 599Large phone24px gutters. QR at min(58vw, 264px). Delta rows become label / value / note on one line. Still one column — width goes into the code, not into a second track.
600 – 899Landscape & tabletCounter splits two-up: QR on the left, note and team code on the right. Body content caps at 560px and centres. Numbers puts the two charts side by side. Tab bar stays at the bottom.
≥ 900RailTab bar becomes a 200px labelled left rail with the full seven destinations. Content caps at 820px. Numbers goes chart-column plus delta-column.
Landscape, h ≤ 460ShortTop bar to 40px, QR panel lays out horizontally, present mode puts the code beside the venue name. Tab targets stay at 48px — a cramped viewport is not a reason to shrink a thumb.
