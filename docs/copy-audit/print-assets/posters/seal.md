# Seal A4 poster copy audit

## Asset context

- **Format:** A4 counter poster
- **Use case:** Curiosity-led counter placement
- **Tone:** Redacted reward manifest
- **Source:** `config/poster-designs.json` → `templates[id="seal"].copy`
- **Placeholders:** `{stamps}`

## Current copy

- **Manifest label:** Reward manifest · Contents undisclosed
- **Headline:** We're not telling.
- **Reward:** Sealed
- **Opens at:** Stamp {stamps} — when your card is full
- **Kept by:** This venue — it won't say what's under the wax
- **Starts:** Today — one scan and today counts as stamp one
- **Friction:** No app — opens in your browser. One text. No password.
  Marketing optional. Stamps go one per UK date.
- **QR caption:** Scan to open the file — today counts as stamp one

## Audit

The visual metaphor and curiosity are excellent. The headline is unnecessarily
adversarial and centres the venue withholding information rather than the
customer earning something. “One scan” overstates the join step. “Open the
file” supports the design concept but does not describe the actual action.

## Recommended hierarchy

1. **Hook:** Your reward is under seal.
2. **Value:** A real reward picked by this venue opens when the card is full.
3. **Progress:** Today's visit can start the card.
4. **Friction:** One text. No app. Marketing optional.
5. **CTA:** Scan to start the file.

## Proposed field rewrite

- **Manifest label:** Reward manifest · Kept under seal
- **Headline:** Your reward is under seal.
- **Reward:** Picked by this venue
- **Opens at:** Stamp {stamps} — when your card is full
- **Kept by:** Your card — hidden until the final stamp
- **Starts:** Complete the join and today's visit can be stamp one
- **Friction:** One text. No app. Marketing optional. One visit stamp per UK
  date.
- **QR caption:** Scan to start your card

## Questions for the next auditor

- Can the redacted-manifest voice remain playful without sounding evasive?
- Should “real reward” be included, or does it defensively introduce doubt?
- Is “picked by this venue” the clearest trust signal?
