# Primer A4 poster copy audit

## Asset context

- **Format:** A4 counter poster
- **Use case:** Considered counter read
- **Tone:** Plain terms and trust
- **Source:** `config/poster-designs.json` → `templates[id="primer"].copy`
- **Placeholders:** `{stamps}`

## Current copy

- **Ledger label:** Loyalty · Statement of terms
- **Headline:** The fair deal starts today.
- **Clause 1:** Scan the QR below
- **Clause 1 detail:** No app — opens in your browser. One text. No password.
  Marketing optional.
- **Clause 2:** Today counts as stamp one
- **Clause 2 detail:** Join at this QR and today's visit counts as stamp one.
- **Clause 3:** One stamp per UK date
- **Clause 3 detail:** A visit today can only be stamped today — tomorrow gets
  its own.
- **Clause 4:** Reward: sealed until stamp {stamps}
- **Clause 4 detail:** Fill your card to have the reward picked and revealed —
  until then, it stays under the wax.
- **QR caption:** Scan to sign — today counts as stamp one

## Audit

The ledger structure builds trust and explains friction well. The headline is
abstract, however, and does not immediately name the customer benefit. “Scan to
sign” adds a legal metaphor when the desired action is simply starting a card.
“Today counts as stamp one” should be qualified because scanning alone does not
issue the stamp.

## Recommended hierarchy

1. **Hook:** Start today. Reveal your reward at stamp {stamps}.
2. **Value:** Fill a loyalty card to reveal a reward picked by this venue.
3. **Progress:** Complete the join and today's visit can be stamp one.
4. **Friction:** One text. No app. Marketing optional.
5. **CTA:** Scan to start your card.

## Proposed field rewrite

- **Headline:** Start today. Reveal your reward at stamp {stamps}.
- **Clause 1 title:** Start your card
- **Clause 1 detail:** Scan below. One text. No app. Marketing optional.
- **Clause 2 title:** Make today stamp one
- **Clause 2 detail:** Complete the join and today's visit can start your card.
- **Clause 3 title:** One visit stamp per UK date
- **Clause 3 detail:** Return on another date to add the next stamp.
- **Clause 4 title:** Your reward stays sealed
- **Clause 4 detail:** Fill the card to reveal a reward picked by this venue.
- **QR caption:** Scan to start your card

## Questions for the next auditor

- Is “fair deal” more trustworthy than the more direct reward-led headline?
- Does “complete the join” sound too procedural for customer copy?
- Can the four clauses be shortened further without losing trust?
