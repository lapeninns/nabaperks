# Regulars A4 table tent copy audit

## Asset context

- **Format:** A4 folded table tent, two customer-facing panels
- **Use case:** Convert a seated customer during the visit
- **Tone:** Familiar and belonging-led
- **Source:** `config/table-tent-designs.json` → `designs[id="regulars"]`
- **Placeholders:** None

## Previous copy

### Face A

- **Headline:** This table / starts your / card.
- **Accent:** card.
- **Body:** Stamp one lands the second you scan. One per UK date — leave without
  scanning and today's is gone.
- **CTA:** Claim before you leave

### Face B

- **Headline:** Regulars keep / a card / here.
- **Accent:** here.
- **Body:** Your card opens from this code and remembers every visit. Fill it
  and the sealed reward opens.
- **CTA:** Scan · Become a regular

### Shared footer

- **Friction:** 10 seconds · No app · No password
- **Left:** One stamp per UK date — today's expires at closing
- **Right:** No app · Opens in your browser

## Audit

The regulars idea offers a strong sense of belonging. Face A overpromises:
scanning does not instantly issue a stamp, and “claim” can sound like an
immediate reward. Face B says the card remembers every visit when it only
records eligible stamps. The ten-second claim is unsupported, and the closing
deadline conflicts with the UK calendar-date rule.

## Recommended hierarchy

1. **Hook:** Make today stamp one.
2. **Value:** Fill the card to reveal a venue-picked reward.
3. **Belonging:** Regulars keep a card here.
4. **Friction:** One text. No app. Marketing optional.
5. **CTA:** Scan to start with today.
