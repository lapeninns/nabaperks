# Classic A4 table tent copy audit

## Asset context

- **Format:** A4 folded table tent, two customer-facing panels
- **Use case:** Bold, minimal conversion message
- **Tone:** Direct and playful
- **Source:** `config/table-tent-designs.json` → `designs[id="classic"]`
- **Placeholders:** None

## Previous copy

### Face A

- **Headline:** Scan. / Stamp. / Don't ask.
- **Body:** We can't tell you what the reward is — that's the point. Stamp one
  starts the moment you scan.
- **CTA:** Point your camera

### Face B

- **Headline:** Stamp one / starts / here.
- **Body:** The rest of the card unlocks the mystery. Staff won't spoil it. We
  won't either.
- **CTA:** Scan to start your card

### Shared footer

- **Friction:** 10 seconds · No app · No password
- **Left:** One stamp per UK date — today's expires at closing
- **Right:** No app · Opens in your browser

## Audit

The short commands are visually strong, but “Don't ask” risks sounding hostile
and withholds the value instead of building useful curiosity. Scanning alone
does not start stamp one. The staff claim is unsupported, while the current
copy never states that the venue has selected a real reward.

## Recommended hierarchy

1. **Hook:** Scan. Start. Come back.
2. **Progress:** Today's visit can be stamp one.
3. **Value:** Fill the card to reveal a venue-picked reward.
4. **Friction:** One text. No app. Marketing optional.
5. **CTA:** Point your camera to start.

## Questions for the next auditor

- Is “Come back” too demanding before the value sentence is read?
