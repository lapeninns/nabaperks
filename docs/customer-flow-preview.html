What this catches

Use the mocks to review copy, missing states, and loop breaks. The highest-risk break is after first join: the customer must not finish account creation and then lose the scan context.

  • Continuity Carry `qrId`, venue, and intended stamp action through OTP and terms.
  • Consent Required terms and optional marketing must remain visibly separate.
  • Recovery Wallet login should recover existing cards only, not create hidden accounts.
Branches customer-only alternate paths and missing-state checks
B.01 ?

Unknown QR

Show a branded error, venue search fallback, and retry. Never drop into a generic join screen.

B.02 ID

Existing member scans

Recognise the contact after OTP and open the current card instead of creating a duplicate.

B.03 OTP

Code delay or rate limit

Give resend timing, edit contact, and calm retry copy. Avoid dead-end account creation.

B.04 LOC

Location unavailable

Soft-check only. Explain why it helps, then allow scan-based eligibility when policy permits.

B.05 DUP

Duplicate same day

Show the last stamp time, next eligible day, and card progress so the customer is not punished.

B.06 RW

Reward blocks stamping

If a reward is ready, make redemption the primary action and explain why new stamps pause.

B.07 LO

Signed out deep link

Return to the exact intended route after OTP: card, stamp confirmation, or reward proof.

B.08 SUP

Lost access

Customer support path should verify contact ownership and recover cards without exposing raw phone data.
