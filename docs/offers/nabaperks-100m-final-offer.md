## 2. The ideal customer

### Built specifically for

Nabaperks should be sold first and foremost to:

- A single-site, owner-operated or owner-led UK pub.
- A venue serving food on its quieter midweek days.
- A pub with visible weekend demand and an existing customer base.
- A business with regular faces but no effective, measurable digital loyalty programme.
- An operator willing to display the supplied QR materials.
- A manager prepared to give staff a short operational brief.
- A venue wanting an implemented programme rather than another empty software account.

The repository contains café, bar and takeaway acquisition surfaces, but the current product positioning is explicitly **pub-first**. Those other verticals should remain secondary until there is stronger vertical-specific proof.

### Not a good fit

Nabaperks should actively disqualify:

- Venues closed most Tuesdays to Thursdays.
- New or persistently quiet venues without an existing customer base.
- Operators expecting guaranteed revenue or guaranteed filled tables.
- Businesses unwilling to display the QR or honour valid rewards.
- Buyers requiring a multi-site subscription, estate-wide administration or bespoke rollout in the standard package.
- Businesses requiring POS/EPOS integration.
- Operators wanting paid advertising management, unlimited consultancy or continuing print budgets.

That disqualification strengthens the offer: Nabaperks is designed to influence repeat behaviour among people already visiting, not manufacture initial demand.

## 3. The problem being solved

A food-led pub can be full on Friday and Saturday while carrying almost the same rent, staffing and kitchen costs through a quiet Tuesday or Wednesday.

Traditional loyalty options tend to introduce another problem:

- Paper cards are easy to distribute but difficult to measure.
- Native apps create download friction.
- POS integrations turn a straightforward scheme into an IT project.
- Generic loyalty software leaves the publican with an empty dashboard and a setup backlog.
- Blanket discounts can damage margins.
- Complex till procedures disappear during the first busy service.
- Manual birthday lists, referral tracking and weekly reporting create more administration.
- Without reliable return-visit data, the operator cannot tell whether the scheme is working.

Nabaperks packages the solution as a managed launch, not simply access to software.

## 4. The Hormozi Value Equation

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort and Sacrifice)**

### Dream outcome: increase it

The real outcome is not “owning loyalty software”. It is:

> **Giving customers who already like the pub a reason to choose it again—and giving the operator a way to see when that happens.**

The programme creates:

- A visible return incentive at the counter.
- A recoverable digital membership rather than a disposable paper card.
- Progress towards food-led mystery rewards.
- Birthday and referral reasons to return.
- A dashboard showing joins, stamps, returning members and redemptions.

The honest boundary is important: Nabaperks encourages and measures return visits; it does not guarantee revenue or table occupancy.

### Perceived likelihood: increase it

The codebase supports four substantial confidence mechanisms:

1. **Done-for-you implementation:** venue, card, rewards, automations and materials are configured as part of the launch.
2. **A tested server-authoritative loyalty loop:** balances and rewards do not depend on a customer screenshot or browser storage.
3. **Merchant-visible measurement:** return behaviour appears in the dashboard and weekly digest.
4. **Two conditional guarantees:** the commercial risk moves towards Nabaperks if the programme does not record the agreed return signals.

The repository does not provide active, reproducible aggregate performance proof, testimonials or venue-specific case studies. Those should not be invented. Until verified evidence exists, the product mechanics, merchant dashboard and guarantees must carry the likelihood argument.

### Time delay: reduce it

The launch follows five guided stages:

1. Share and configure the venue.
2. Review the branded loyalty card.
3. Confirm the pre-filled reward pool.
4. Activate billing and the platform pilot.
5. Approve and launch the permanent venue QR.

This removes the blank-account problem. The offer should not promise an unsupported “five-minute setup” or guaranteed same-day launch, especially because physical printing and delivery are involved.

### Effort and sacrifice: reduce them

For customers:

- No native app.
- No Apple or Google Wallet pass.
- No password to remember.
- A phone-based verification journey.
- A card that reopens in the browser.

For merchants:

- No POS/EPOS integration.
- No additional till hardware.
- A pre-configured reward pool.
- Printed launch materials supplied.
- Birthday and referral logic configured.
- Weekly reporting delivered automatically.
- A permanent QR rather than repeated campaign setup.

## 5. The core offer stack

### Deliverable 1: Done-for-you venue and loyalty-card launch

Nabaperks configures:

- The pub’s venue profile and address.
- Its branded browser loyalty card.
- A recommended five-stamp, food-led cycle.
- The permanent venue QR.
- The customer join and repeat-stamp journey.
- Optional venue location checks.
- Billing activation and launch readiness.

This means the merchant begins with a working programme rather than an empty software account.

### Deliverable 2: Margin-conscious mystery reward pool

The standard launch includes a custom weighted reward pool. The code requires at least three active rewards before the live QR is launch-ready.

Suggested reward territory includes:

- A complimentary starter.
- Coffee after lunch.
- A Sunday-roast upgrade.
- Other venue-approved, food-led rewards.

The merchant retains control of:

- Reward name.
- Terms and exclusions.
- Weighting.
- Display order.
- Active status.
- Stamp target.

This solves “what should I give away?” without positioning blanket discounting as the answer.

### Deliverable 3: Permanent no-app venue QR

Customers scan a venue-specific QR and use the loyalty card in their browser.

The underlying journey supports:

- Phone OTP verification.
- Loyalty-terms acceptance.
- Membership creation.
- Eligible first-stamp issuance.
- Returning-customer stamping.
- Saved customer sessions and card recovery.
- Customer card, reward, activity and profile views.

Normal visit stamps are restricted to one per customer, venue location and Europe/London business date. Optional location checks provide evidence, but should not be sold as impossible-to-defeat fraud prevention.

### Deliverable 4: Reward issuance and secure collection

When the configured target is reached, Nabaperks creates a reward record.

The implemented collection process includes:

- A customer reward wallet.
- Reward terms and availability status.
- Profile and eligibility checks.
- A short-lived reward QR token.
- Merchant-side authenticated scanning.
- Single-use collection.
- A redemption ledger.
- The next stamp cycle opening after collection.

Cycle rewards are currently held until the next eligible UK weekday, skipping Saturday and Sunday. Customers must complete their profile, be 18 or over and verify an email before producing the redemption QR. These are real product conditions and should be visible during merchant onboarding.

### Deliverable 5: Merchant measurement

The merchant dashboard and reporting surfaces cover:

- Members.
- New members.
- Stamps.
- Visits.
- Returning customers.
- Rewards issued.
- Rewards redeemed.
- Activity history.
- Customer membership readback.
- QR downloads.
- Weekly email summaries.

This turns loyalty from an unmeasured paper exercise into a system the operator can inspect.

## 6. The bonus stack

These are the three bonuses supported by the current offer source. They should be presented individually because each removes a distinct obstacle.

### Bonus 1: Print-ready marketing—first run printed and posted

**Obstacle removed:** “I have no designer and no time to organise printing.”

Nabaperks:

- Generates A4 counter materials using the pub’s venue QR.
- Applies the existing Nabaperks print design system and counter copy.
- Physically prints and posts the first run to the venue.

**Defensible anchor:** The repository uses a £150+ comparison for what a freelance designer and local print shop could charge to design, produce and fulfil comparable materials.

This first run is already part of the £299.99 launch. It must not be presented as a temporary promotional freebie.

### Bonus 2: Birthday automation, configured

**Obstacle removed:** “Nobody will remember to message birthday customers.”

Nabaperks configures:

- Whether birthday rewards are enabled.
- The reward name.
- Reward terms.
- Automated issuance during a member’s birthday month.
- Expiry at the end of the applicable month.
- Standard wallet and redemption handling.

The value is the recurring manual task it removes, not a fabricated monetary valuation.

### Bonus 3: “Bring a Regular” referrals, configured

**Obstacle removed:** “I want customers to introduce new diners without paying for ads.”

Existing members receive a shareable referral link. Nabaperks handles:

- Referral attribution.
- Membership linking.
- Qualification.
- Bonus settlement.
- Duplicate and self-referral controls.
- Referral operations and support review.

The referrer’s bonus settles only after the invited customer completes a genuine visit. Invitation or bonus stamps do not qualify as that genuine visit.

### Included, but not double-counted as bonuses

These are already part of the Growth Plan:

- Weekly digest.
- Consent handling.
- Mystery-reward presets.
- Unlimited stamps and members.
- Permanent venue QR.
- Optional location checks.

Counting them again as separate high-value bonuses would weaken the offer’s credibility.

## 8. Risk reversal

### Guarantee 1: First-Regular Guarantee

> **If your live card has not brought back a first regular by the end of the 28-day pilot, the platform pilot stays free until it does.**

A first regular is defined in the merchant terms as a member receiving another normal visit stamp on a later Europe/London date.

The guarantee:

- Applies from the day the venue QR goes live.
- Requires the merchant to contact the Nabaperks team.
- Is fulfilled through a manually applied pilot extension.
- Is not an automatic refund or cash payment.

Conditions:

- The done-for-you setup must be completed.
- Approved posters must be displayed.
- Rewards must be honoured.
- The programme must not be deliberately gamed by staff.

### Guarantee 2: 90-Day ROI Extension

> **If the loyalty card does not record 18 verified return visits within 90 days, you receive £209.97 of plan-fee relief.**

Mechanically:

- Three payments equal £209.97.
- At the offer’s illustrative £25 contribution per return visit, 18 visits contribute approximately £450.
- If the dashboard has not recorded 18 verified returns, support applies three fully discounted renewal invoices on 28-day billing or returns £209.97 from the current annual subscription payment.

Conditions:

- The QR remains actively displayed throughout the 90 days.
- Rewards are honoured.
- Test activity and staff gaming are excluded.
- The merchant must contact Nabaperks for the discount.

#### Important commercial alignment issue

The 90-Day ROI Extension appears in the active marketing facts and public guarantee presentation, but the current merchant terms explicitly document only the First-Regular Guarantee. Before using the ROI Extension in contracts or sales, its starting date, claim window, interaction with an extended pilot and discount fulfilment should be added to the merchant terms.

### What is never guaranteed

Nabaperks does not guarantee:

- Midweek revenue.
- Filled tables.
- A particular return rate.
- A specific profit figure.
- Complete prevention of fraud.
- Legal or regulatory compliance merely through use of the product.

That boundary should appear beside the guarantees, not in hidden small print.

## 9. Price and value mathematics

### Standard Growth Plan

**Pay today:**

- £299.99 one-time, per-venue launch fee.
- Covers venue and card configuration, reward setup, bespoke printed launch materials and delivery.

**Then:**

- 28-day free platform pilot.
- Payment card required.
- No recurring platform payment during the standard pilot.

**After the pilot:**

- £69.99 every 28 days.
- Thirteen recurring payments in each 364-day billing year.
- £909.87 total recurring cost per 364-day billing year.
- No separate card-processing surcharge.
- Cancel through the billing page.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

From checkout through the first 364 days, the code’s value model assumes the £299.99 launch plus 12 post-pilot payments: **£1,139.87**, unless the merchant cancels or receives a guarantee extension.

### Illustrative break-even framing

The approved offer uses a hypothetical **£25 contribution per additional return visit after costs**.

On that assumption:

- Around eight additional profitable return visits every 28 days cover the launch and post-pilot payments across the first 364 days.
- After the launch year, around six additional profitable visits every 28 days cover the £69.99 recurring payment.
- Eighteen return visits contribute approximately £450, more than three payments totalling £209.97.

This is illustrative, not a customer result. Each pub’s margins and average transaction values differ, and the merchant should use its dashboard and actual contribution margin.

### Bespoke alternative

**The Ultimate Pub Loyalty Takeover — £4,999.99**

- Enquiry only.
- No self-serve checkout.
- Scope must be agreed before purchase.
- No unspecified agency services should be implied.

This functions as a genuine bespoke alternative and price anchor, not as a fake “normally £4,999.99” valuation of the Growth Plan.

## 10. Honest scarcity and urgency

### Capacity

> **Nabaperks onboards only five new pubs per week.**

The reason is operational:

- Each venue is configured by a human team.
- Rewards are prepared with the venue.
- Physical materials must be generated, printed and fulfilled.
- Five is the current stated capacity for completing those launches properly.

If a week is full, the venue is moved to the next available opening.

### Urgency

> **Confirm this week and the launch joins the next physical print batch, so the posters arrive and the programme can go live sooner.**

There should be:

- No fake countdown.
- No invented “spots left” counter.
- No artificial deadline.
- No claim of current availability until confirmed by a human.

## 12. Merchant responsibilities and material limitations

The merchant must:

- Provide accurate venue information.
- Approve the card and rewards.
- Maintain at least three active rewards while the live QR is enabled.
- Display the supplied materials.
- Brief authorised staff.
- Honour valid rewards.
- Define clear reward terms and exclusions.
- Maintain secure merchant and billing access.
- Avoid test or staff gaming.

Material product limitations:

- The standard offer is per venue, not a multi-site estate package.
- The product needs network access; it is not an offline stamping ledger.
- Redemption requires an authenticated merchant scanning device.
- Normal stamps are limited to one per venue location per UK date.
- Location checks are soft evidence, not a perfect anti-fraud barrier.
- Customers must be 18 or over and have a verified email before reward collection.
- Billing must remain active or trialling. Once billing is cancelled, past due, unpaid, paused or otherwise blocked, joins, stamps, reward issue and redemption are paused.
- Ongoing poster reprints are not included as an unlimited budget.
- Paid advertising, POS integrations, monthly strategy calls and unlimited consulting are excluded.

## 14. Recommended sales-page sequence

The offer should be presented in this order:

1. **Outcome-led hero:** give the weekend crowd a reason to return on Tuesday.
2. **Mechanism:** one counter QR, a browser card and no POS integration.
3. **Qualification:** explicitly state who the launch is and is not for.
4. **Problem:** explain why generic software, paper cards and native apps fail at the counter.
5. **Done-for-you launch:** show the five launch stages.
6. **Core stack:** card, reward pool, QR, redemption and reporting.
7. **Bonus stack:** printed materials, birthdays and Bring a Regular.
8. **Transformation:** from unmeasured quiet-midweek guesswork to a system the operator can watch.
9. **Price:** launch fee, free pilot, recurring cadence and full billing disclosure.
10. **Guarantees:** display their mechanics and conditions as prominently as their promise lines.
11. **Scarcity:** state the genuine five-launch weekly capacity and print-batch timing.
12. **FAQ and objection handling.**
13. **Single primary action:** start the launch.

The root landing page can remain shorter, with the deeper stack spread across pricing, how-it-works and FAQ pages, as the current route architecture intends.

## 15. Claims that must not enter the offer

Do not claim that Nabaperks:

- Guarantees revenue or filled tables.
- Makes fraud impossible.
- Prevents all self-stamping.
- Is legally approved, certified or guaranteed compliant.
- Launches in an unsupported number of minutes.
- Guarantees a first repeat visit in the first week.
- Has a particular number of live venues without reproducible evidence.
- Has only a particular number of “spots left” without a live reservation ledger.
- Produces a proven average return rate without an approved underlying dataset.

The safest persuasive language is tied to delivery facts and merchant-visible signals: the programme is configured, the materials are produced, return visits are recorded and the guarantees respond to those recorded signals.

## 16. Final strategic assessment

The codebase supports a strong Hormozi-style offer because Nabaperks is not merely selling loyalty software. It combines:

- A narrow, economically recognisable pub problem.
- A defined customer avatar.
- Done-for-you implementation.
- Physical and digital fulfilment.
- Low customer adoption friction.
- A measurable return signal.
- Two conditional risk reversals.
- Real operational scarcity.
- Transparent pricing and cancellation.

Its weakest current lever is external proof. The repository does not support publishing credible testimonials, venue uplift, a verified aggregate repeat rate or production-provider readiness. The next major commercial improvement should therefore be a reproducible evidence system and approved case studies—not a larger feature stack.

Before using the complete offer in a contract or live sales process, align the 90-Day ROI Extension with the merchant terms and make its timing and fulfilment rules explicit.
