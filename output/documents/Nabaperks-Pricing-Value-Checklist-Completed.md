**Nabaperks completed worksheet**  
**Niche:** Established, owner-operated UK gastropubs with underused Tuesday-Thursday capacity  
**Date:** 18 July 2026

## III. Charge What It's Worth

### New price

> **£1,500 per venue for the 90-day done-for-you launch, then £249/month managed.**

- [x] **Be more expensive than generic loyalty software**

  Remove the public £49 comparison. Sell the managed offer against the cost of underused midweek capacity, not against an internal cheap tier.

- [x] **High price must create higher value**

  The £1,500 launch funds the retention audit, reward design, done-for-you configuration, QR assets, staff briefing, launch sprint, campaign calendar and 30/60/90-day reviews.

- [x] **Higher price should improve results**

  Qualification, named ownership, implementation, accountability and proactive optimisation justify the price. Price alone does not create customer success.

## IV. Maximize Value Using the Value Equation

- [x] **Maximize Dream Outcome**

  Build a measurable base of regulars returning Tuesday-Thursday and help recover the £1,500 launch investment through tracked gross contribution from eligible verified midweek returning-member visits.

- [x] **Maximize Perceived Likelihood of Success**

  Use operator credibility, first-party repeat evidence, done-for-you implementation, a Midweek Return Dashboard, baseline measurement, campaign attribution, 30/60/90-day targets and weekly next-action reporting.

- [x] **Minimize Time to Success**

  Show the working card and QR in 15 minutes; prepare approved assets within two business days; run the Day-One 10 Member Sprint; target the first verified midweek return within seven days. Ninety days is the campaign horizon, not the first win.

- [x] **Minimize Effort & Sacrifice**

  Nabaperks configures the card, rewards, QR, assets, staff briefing, launch messages, campaign calendar and reporting. The venue only supplies accurate inputs, approves and honours rewards, displays the QR and names one decision-maker.
